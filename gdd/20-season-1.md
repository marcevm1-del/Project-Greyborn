# 20 — Season 1: The Hush Wakes

A full plan for the first season of the Answering War ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)).
The model is decided (community goals, option A); the content is a proposal.

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 10 weeks |
| **Fronts** | Ashen Steppe (Ashfall Crossing), Bone Flats (The Elder Ribs), Rootwilds (Breathing Canopy) |
| **Story** | Something is stirring at the edge of the Starwound. As the season goes on, signs grow that one of the Hushed Answers, the **Glass Range** (the Murmur's copy of the Mountain That Walked), is waking |
| **Finale** | A limited-time mode, **The Glass Range Walks** (week 10) |

## Why community goals are fair

Every match has exactly **one Wildborn team and one Blightborn team**. Both
sides therefore play the same number of matches, and the race between them
is fair without any scaling. The only thing designers need to balance is that
each front's map comes up equally often in rotation.

## Front goals

Each front has three goal actions per side. They are **mirrors**, with the
side's flavour. Every action in a match earns **front points** for that
side on the front where the match was played.

| Front | Wildborn actions | Blightborn actions | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ashen Steppe** | Rally creatures · purge Blight cells · win fights in the Underroot tunnels during the Migration | Infect creatures · consume root cells · win fights in the tunnels during the Migration | Control the herds |
| **Bone Flats** | Find Memory sites · break weak points · destroy Enemy Cores | Find Memory sites · break weak points · destroy Enemy Cores | The Murmur hunts the planet's memory; the planet guards it |
| **Rootwilds** | Protect Sapback Aphid herds · capture nodes during canopy-fall · purge the Weavemother's webs | Destroy Aphid herds (or infect them) · capture nodes during canopy-fall · keep webs on enemy nodes | The planet's lungs |

**Front target:** each side needs to reach a fixed total of front points (set
after beta data). If one side reaches the target first, that's their win on
that front. If both reach it in the same week, the front is **Contested**.

|---|---|---|

both sides. Each result also unlocks a themed cosmetic set for everyone.

## Finale mode: The Glass Range Walks

A limited-time **objective mode** in week 10. It stays 4v4 and stays fair,
because teams play both sides.

### Setup
- Map: a special version of **Ashfall Crossing**.
- The **Glass Range** (the Hushed copy of the Mountain That Walked) walks a
  fixed path from the crater zone toward the Heartwood grove.
- **Two rounds.** Teams swap sides between them.

### Objectives
- **Blightborn: escort.** Keep the Glass Range walking. It moves only while
  the nodes along its path are Blightborn territory. Blight-capture ahead of
  it to keep it moving.
- **Wildborn: stop it.** The Glass Range has **three crystal hearts** (weak
  points) on its back, shoulders and chest. Break all three before it reaches
  the grove. Rooting nodes in its path also stops it.
- The Glass Range attacks only **Wildborn**: slow, huge, telegraphed stomps.

### Winning
- Each round is scored by **how far** the Glass Range walked (Blightborn) or
  **how early** the hearts broke (Wildborn).
- The team with the better combined result over both rounds wins.
- Every match adds to a **community finale total**. If the Wildborn stop it
  more often, the Glass Range **retreats** into the Starwound (a Memory for
  Season 2). If the Blightborn escort it further on average, it **stays
  standing** at the edge of the Ashen Steppe as a landmark in Season 2.

### Why this design
- It uses existing systems (weak points, node capture, escort pacing) instead of new rules.
- It stays PvP, so it's still competitive. Both teams play both roles.
- It pays off the season story with something huge and visible.
