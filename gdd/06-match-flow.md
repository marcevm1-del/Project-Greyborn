# 06 — Match Flow (4v4)

> Everything in this file is a **proposal** that ties the source pages together.
> The source shows four game states (in the Synergy Access Matrix) but not their
> timings or the win condition.

## Pre-match: sides and draft

0. Each team is **assigned a side** for the match: **Wildborn** (the planet's
   response) or **Blightborn** (the Murmur's infected). The sides are mirrors
   in gameplay (see [10](10-world.md)).
1. Teams draft lineages in alternating picks (1-2-2-2-1). No duplicates within a team.
2. Each player's lineage is **hidden from the enemy** until it awakens at Level 3.
   The enemy sees only four identical humanoid Base Forms.
3. Draft strategy: two full pairs (strong synergy), or one pair plus two
   singles (more flexible, but fewer Synergies).

## Phases

| # | Phase (from the matrix) | Time (proposal) | What happens | Synergy rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stable Flow** | 0:00–5:00 | Spread out, root nearby nodes, early skirmishes, lineages awaken | All tiers normal |
| 2 | **Pre-Aggro Resource Control** | 5:00–10:00 | Fights over the 5 Resource Hubs; first base returns; Stage 2 at about minute 8–10 | All tiers normal |
| 3 | **Resource Stage** | 10:00–16:00 | Hub Defense race, Enemy Core sieges, first global events | S1 locked · S2 31 s · S3 20 s |
| 4 | **Hunt** | 16:00–end | Ultimate Forms, decisive teamfights, base push | S3 only (9 s) |

Phase changes are announced globally and change the map. For example, at
Phase 3 the central Hub's roots become permanently Exposed. At Phase 4, every
node next to a base becomes Vulnerable, which opens the bases to attack.

## Win conditions (proposal)

1. **Destroy the enemy Base Heart** (attackable only in Phase 4, or earlier if
   all three of that team's Enemy Cores are destroyed).
2. **Time limit, 25:00:** the team with the higher **Territorial Influence** wins.
3. **Mercy rule:** TI ≥ 80% for 60 consecutive seconds is an instant win.

## Respawn

- Respawn at base after **5 s + 1 s per level** (max 25 s at Level 20).
- You **lose carried cores** (they drop where you died) but keep your EXP and level.
- Respawning at base does **not** auto-convert carried cores. You have to stay
  alive and channel.

## A sample match beat

> 07:40. The Titan and Brawler are carrying 260 cores each. Returning now
> means a +20% bonus but leaves the centre Hub undefended. The Hollow pings a
> Rootquake at 85 Tension nearby. They take one more fight: Quake Slam →
> Roll Commit. They're under Level 18, so it's not a Synergy yet, but it lands
> anyway. The enemy Thornrunner drops 190 cores. Now each is carrying 300+,
> which shows them on the enemy minimap. The run home is the most dangerous 20
> seconds of the match.

This is the core loop the source pages describe: **kills → carry → risky return → evolve → dominate the map.**

## A match, minute by minute

This walkthrough follows one brood through a full match, so every system can
be seen working together. Times follow the proposed phases above.

**0:00 — Budding.** The match opens in darkness with a single heartbeat. The
birth-node cluster at each base (the Cradle for Wildborn, the Clutch for
Blightborn, [33](33-structures.md)) splits open, and four small Kith step out
into their side's colours: amber light on moss-flecked skin, or cyan veins
under a glaze of glass. For the first few seconds there is no HUD pressure and
no enemy in sight, only the sound of the map: wind across the Ashen Steppe,
the canopy breathing in the Rootwilds, bones rattling on the Bone Flats. This
quiet opening is deliberate. It tells players where they are before it asks
them to fight.

**0:15 – 3:00 — Spreading out.** The brood splits into its natural pairs. One
pair heads for the nearest Resource Hub, the other for the cheap nodes close
to base. Base Forms are fast and fragile, so early fights are skirmishes over
wildlife and loose nodes rather than real teamfights. The first evolution
cores come from Gravel Skinks, Glimmerfoxes and Strays, and from any careless
enemy Kith caught alone. Players learn the map's rhythm here: when the herds
move, where the scavengers gather, which Memory site is close enough to visit.

**~3:00 — Awakening.** Most players reach Level 3 around now, and lineages
awaken. This is the first big reveal of the match. Until this moment, enemies
have seen only identical humanoid Base Forms; now, one by one, silhouettes
change and sounds change with them. A Titan's first heavy footfall, a
Thornrunner's skitter, a Hollow's pressing silence. Both teams learn the
enemy draft at the same time, and plans change.

**5:00 — Phase 2, Pre-Aggro Resource Control.** A low horn-like tone (from
the planet) or a rising chime (from the Murmur) marks the phase change, and
the music adds rhythm. Resource Hubs become the focus. The first players
start carrying 150+ cores, and the carry signature makes them audible. The
first real decision of the match arrives: return now for a small bonus, or
push one more fight.

**8:00 – 10:00 — Stage 2.** The first players hit Level 10 and transform. On
the Wildborn side, roots burst from the ground and wrap the creature; on the
Blightborn side, a crystal cocoon forms and shatters. Both are heard across
the map. Each player chooses a branch (Aggression or Tactical Control, [31](31-evolution-branches.md)),
and the match's shape becomes visible: a team that went heavy Aggression will
look for fights, a Tactical team will build territory.

**10:00 — Phase 3, Resource Stage.** Synergy cooldowns jump (31 s / 20 s),
pushing teams away from constant teamfights and toward the economy. This is
when Enemy Cores are sieged, Hub Defense levels climb, and the first global
events fire as borders reach 100 Tension. If the losing side's patron
intervenes, the whole map feels it: a deep exhale from the ground, or a wall
of whispers.

**12:00 – 15:00 — Synergies come online.** Verdant + Hollow unlock their Void
Garden at Level 12; the Hunt pair's Co-Stalk follows at Level 15. Fights
become about pairs: who has their partner close, who has their Synergy ready.
The call wheel ([25](25-calls-and-onboarding.md)) fills the air with "Slam up",
"Marked" and "Garden" calls.

**16:00 — Phase 4, the Hunt.** Only S3 Synergies can fire now. Every node
next to each base becomes Vulnerable, opening the bases. The two musical
themes, wood and glass, collide. Ultimate Forms appear at Level 20, and the
map holds a handful of towering Ascendants whose every step can be heard.

**16:00 – end — The push.** Fights decide the match: a Smash & Roll that
launches the enemy Verdant, a Co-Stalk that breaks a Titan's crystal spine, a
Bone Cathedral planted against a Heartseed. The match ends when a Base Heart
falls, when the mercy rule triggers, or at 25:00 on Territorial Influence.

## Phase transitions in detail

Each phase change is a moment the whole match shares. It has four parts:

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Sound** | A map-wide cue in the player's own side palette: a deep horn of wood for Wildborn, a ringing chime for Blightborn |
| **Sky** | The light shifts: dusk deepens, or the Fall-line brightens, so the phase reads even with the sound off |
| **Map change** | The phase's rule (central Hub exposed in Phase 3, base-adjacent nodes Vulnerable in Phase 4) happens with a visible event: roots cracking open, glass splitting |
| **HUD** | A short banner with the phase name and its one key rule ("Synergies slowed", "Bases are open") |

## How a match ends

The ending should feel like the world itself reacting, not just a scoreboard.

- **Victory by Base Heart:** the winning team's material floods the enemy base.
  A Wildborn victory shows roots tearing through the Clutch and moss covering
  the glass. A Blightborn victory shows the Cradle's root knots glazing over,
  one by one, until the birth-pool turns cyan.
- **Victory on time:** the camera pulls up over the map, showing the final
  territory as amber and cyan regions, then settles on the winning brood.
- **Defeat:** the losing brood is shown together, still standing. They aren't
  humiliated; they lie down together as Kith do, and the soil takes them back.
  Greyborn's tone stays dignified for both sides.
- **The post-match screen** shows front points earned for the War Map, Memories
  found, Mastery progress and Pair Bond progress, in that order. The war comes first.

## Pacing targets

| Moment | Target time | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| First lineage awakens | 2:30 – 3:30 | The first reveal; keeps early play short |
| First return to base | 5:00 – 7:00 | The first risky decision |
| First Stage 2 | 8:00 – 10:00 | A visible power jump before Phase 3 |
| First global event | 10:00 – 13:00 | The map comes alive mid-match |
| First Synergy | 12:00 – 14:00 | Pairs become the focus |
| First Level 20 | 16:00 – 18:00 | Ultimate Forms arrive for the Hunt |
| Average match end | 20:00 – 23:00 | Inside the 18–25 minute target |
