# 44 — Flora of Greyborn

[12](12-life-on-greyborn.md#flora-plants-players-interact-with) introduced 9
interactable plants. This chapter expands the catalogue to **26 plants and
fungi** across every region, with their Blighted and Scalded forms. All are proposals.

**Types:** **Interactable** plants do something in a match. **Ambient** plants
are scenery that makes a region recognisable (no gameplay effect).

## The original nine (summary)

Sapbloom (heal), Thunderpod (knockback), Veilfern (hide), Bonevine (climb),
Lanternmoss (tripwire light), Rimecap (freeze patch), Embergrass (fire
trail), Glasscap (Blightborn heal / Wildborn damage), Heartbloom (Wildborn
heal / Blightborn damage). See [12](12-life-on-greyborn.md).

## Blighted and Scalded forms

Plants change when the Murmur or the Clamor takes the ground.

| Effect | Blighted (Murmur) | Scalded (Clamor) |
|---|---|---|
| **General look** | Leaves turn to glass, flowers to crystal that never open | Leaves blacken and curl; stems crack and glow like coals |
| **Interactables** | Still work, with the same rules, but in Blight colours. Glasscap grows instead of Sapbloom | **Stop working**: Scald kills them. They regrow after the Scald burns out |
| **Ambient** | Glass versions (e.g. glass Ashgrass that chimes in the wind) | Ash and char |

**Why Scald stops plants but Blight doesn't:** the Murmur *copies*, so its
plants keep their shape and function. The Clamor *devours*, so it leaves
nothing behind. This makes Clamor Landfalls ([22](22-season-3.md)) more
threatening: they strip the map of its tools.
