# 07 — Tactical Notes (Chapter 7)

The source sidebar lists four kinds of tactical note: **Evolution/Global Event
Synergy tracks**, **Global/Dynamic Resource Control Strategies** (*"advice on
node management for stabilising or unstable globals for global evolution"*),
**Return to base** (*"advice on evolution order"*) and **coordinated
interdependent abilities**. This chapter is written as player-facing
strategy, which doubles as a check on whether the systems produce real decisions.

## 1. Node management: stabilise or destabilise

- **Stabilising (when you're ahead):** root nodes *behind* your border first.
  They can't be Vulnerable while connected only to your own territory, so they
  give safe SAP and passive growth. Upgrade Hub Defense before you expand.
- **Destabilising (when you're behind):** cut connections. Uprooting a single
  "bridge" node can turn several enemy nodes **Inaccessible to them for
  reinforcement** and Vulnerable to you (pre-attack vs. post-attack).
- Keep a border near an event your pair is good at, and let Tension build there on purpose.

## 2. Return to base: evolution order

- **The 300 rule:** returning at 300+ carried cores gets the full +30% bonus.
  Below 100, a return usually isn't worth the map time unless a Stage threshold is close.
- **Return in pairs.** Evolution-Sync Resonance (+10% EXP within 2 levels)
  and the free Synergy level for reaching a Stage together are worth more than
  one partner rushing ahead.
- **Who evolves first?** In general: the pair whose Synergy unlocks earliest
  (Verdant + Hollow at 12) gets priority in the early game, and the Commit pair
  (18) in the late game.
- **Field-convert when your base is far** and a Hub is safe: 70% now often
  beats 100% after a 40-second round trip.

## 3. Synergy tracks: playing the matrix

- **Phases 1–2:** farm Synergy *levels* (fight near your partner) even before
  the ability unlocks. Reaching S3 by Phase 4 decides whether you can use
  Synergies at all in the endgame.
- **Phase 3:** Synergy cooldowns jump to 31 s / 20 s. Spend this phase on
  Hubs, SAP and Enemy Cores, not on chasing teamfights.
- **Phase 4:** only S3 pairs can fire. A team with two S3 pairs has a large advantage.

## 4. Coordinated interdependent abilities (callouts)

| Callout | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **"Slam up"** | Titan's Quake Slam is ready. Brawler, be within roll range |
| **"Marked"** | Thornrunner has marked a target. Bonespire, lance within 4 s |
| **"Garden"** | Hollow is about to Rift on Verdant's root. Fight inside the field |
| **"Heavy"** | You're carrying 300+ and visible on their minimap. Request an escort home |
| **"Tension"** | A border is close to 100. Rotate if the event favours your pair |

## 5. Counterplay quick reference

| Threat | Counter |
|---|---|
| Smash & Roll | Spread out. A Hollow Null Pulse on the Titan before the slam lands. Bait the Titan into slamming alone (its cooldown +50%) |
| Co-Stalk | Break line of sight with the Bonespire after you're Marked. Titan Bulwark covers a frontal weak point |
| Void Garden | Fight just outside it. Ranged poke (Bonespire) into the field. Kill the Verdant first: no roots, no field |
| A player carrying a lot | Thornrunner's Hunt Frenzy shows anyone carrying 200+. Intercept on their route home |

## 6. Priorities by lineage

How each lineage should think about the match at each stage.

| Lineage | Early (L1–9) | Mid (L10–15) | Late (L16–20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Titan** | Survive; take safe nodes near base; stay close to the Brawler | Hold Hubs with Bulwark; set up slams for the Brawler | Be the wall in front of your team; Shatterfist or Fortress decides fights |
| **Brawler** | Hunt lone Base Forms and Strays for cores | Dive carriers on their way home; Grapple players off nodes | Commit with the Titan; Frenzy or Brawl Circle to force fights |
| **Verdant** | Root everything near base fast (2 s channel) | Build the network: SAP extraction, territory growth | Overgrowth or Living Wall to swing the map; protect the Hollow |
| **Hollow** | Stay hidden; take mire and cave nodes | Void Rift on Verdant ground (Void Garden from L12) | Event Horizon to pull the enemy into your team's Synergies |
| **Thornrunner** | Farm wildlife on the flanks; scout | Mark carriers; intercept returns | Hunt Frenzy to find heavy carriers; Gut the Hoard |
| **Bonespire** | Poke from range; take high ground | Break weak points; siege Enemy Cores | Bone Cathedral against Hubs and Cores; Rememberer's Aim in teamfights |
| **Stillheart** | Escort carriers; Lull divers | Hibernate allies who carry a lot | Long Night and The Deep Sleep to freeze the map at the decisive moment |

## 7. Reading the map: signals every player should know

The living world constantly tells observant players what is happening.

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| A flock of Strider Cranes taking off | Someone just moved through the mire nearby |
| Ash Vultures circling | A fight is happening below, or about to |
| Marrowhounds running in one direction | Something died there in the last 30 seconds |
| Birdsong stops | A Hollow may be close ([43](43-living-creatures.md)) |
| Prey fleeing with no visible predator | A Thornrunner is hunting |
| A deep tremor or ringing chime from one direction | Someone just reached a new Stage |
| A rising drone in a region | Tension is past 75; a global event is coming |
| Lanternmoss lighting up | Something walked past it |

## 8. Common mistakes (and what to do instead)

1. **Carrying too long.** Holding 400 cores to squeeze out the last bonus
   makes you the loudest creature on the map. Return at 300.
2. **Fighting in Phase 3 like it's Phase 2.** Synergy cooldowns jump in the
   Resource Stage. Use that phase for Hubs and Cores.
3. **Ignoring wildlife.** Leaving Strays alone near your territory lets them
   grow into a Wild Ascendant ([46](46-wild-ascendants.md)), and overhunting a region
   makes its predators hostile ([15](15-ecology.md#overhunting)). Manage the wildlife; don't ignore it.
4. **Splitting a pair.** A Titan without its Brawler loses its slam cooldown
   on every lonely cast. Pairs should rotate together.
5. **Chasing the last hit on a Landfall core.** If both teams are fighting the
   Clamor, the team that stops to fight each other often loses the reward to the third party: the Scald.
6. **Forgetting the comeback events.** If your team is far ahead, the enemy's
   patron is about to intervene. Don't overextend into a Planet Pulse or a Murmur Surge.

## 9. Example team plans

- **Two pairs, territory focus** (Verdant, Hollow, Thornrunner, Bonespire):
  win the map early, use Void Garden to lock Hubs, Co-Stalk to remove divers.
- **Two pairs, fight focus** (Titan, Brawler, Thornrunner, Bonespire): take
  every fight, deny enemy evolution through kills, close the match early in Phase 4.
- **One pair plus Stillheart** (Titan, Brawler, Stillheart, Verdant): a slow,
  safe team that protects carriers with Hibernate and wins on Territorial Influence at time.
