# 02 — World & Lore

## The world: the Pall

The Pall is a continent-sized ruin of what was once **Chroma**, a land where
colour and soul were the same thing. Today it is almost entirely greyscale:
grey skies, grey stone, grey people. Colour survives only where something
living still clings to its Hue: a burning beast, a flooded shrine, a sick
grove that will not die.

### Rules of the world (these must stay consistent)

1. **Every living soul has exactly one Hue.** It is that creature's nature *and* its magic.
2. **Hue can be taken.** Kill a Hued creature while it is "flared" and its Hue
   tears loose as a visible **Wisp**. Normally the Wisp drifts away and fades.
3. **The Greyborn have no Hue,** so a Greyborn can catch a Wisp and hold it. No
   one else can.
4. **Holding a Hue that isn't yours changes you** (Stain). A Greyborn who holds
   too much becomes a **Husk**, a body that belongs to the colour and not the person.
5. **The land's colour is stored in Prism Hearts**, one per Hue, each guarded by
   the creature that swallowed it during the Bleaching.

## History

| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| **The Spectrum Age** (~1000 years ago) | Six Hue-kingdoms flourish, each devoted to one colour. Hue marriages create mixed "Blend" bloodlines. |
| **The Choir's Rise** (~400 years ago) | The **White Choir**, an ascetic order, teaches that Hue is desire, desire is suffering, and only White (all colours united and stilled) is peace. |
| **The Bleaching** (~300 years ago) | The Choir sings the **Unmaking Hymn** across all six capitals at once. Colour drains from the land. Hues flee into beasts, monsters and a few desperate heroes, who become the region guardians. The kingdoms collapse. |
| **The Long Grey** (300 years → now) | The **Pale Church** (the Choir's successor) rules the surviving grey cities. Shortly after the Bleaching, Greyborn children start being born. The Church calls them *omens* and takes them to be "returned to White". |
| **Present day** | The Hymn is failing. Hued beasts are growing wilder and spreading. The Church is quietly gathering Greyborn faster than ever. |

## The central secret (the act 3 reveal)

The Unmaking Hymn **did not finish.** It scattered colour instead of uniting it.
The Greyborn are the Hymn's own echo: *empty vessels the world made to gather
the scattered colour back up.* The Pale Church knows this. It plans to have one
Greyborn collect every Hue and then sacrifice them in the White Cathedral to
finish the Hymn and bleach the world permanently.

**The player character is that Greyborn.** The Church's "pursuit" of you has
always been herding. The Hues you collect and the Prism Hearts you restore are
exactly what the Church needs.

The player learns this after restoring the 4th Prism Heart (see [06 — Narrative](06-narrative.md)).
It turns their main progression into a moral question: *keep restoring colour,
knowing you are becoming the perfect offering?*

## Factions

### The Pale Church (antagonist institution)
- Rulers of the grey cities; white robes, featureless porcelain masks.
- Believes sincerely that colour causes suffering. Most of its clergy are
  not villains. They think the Bleaching *saved* people from war.
- Military arm: **the Bleached Knights**, soldiers who have had their own Hue
  ritually drained. They are immune to Hue-stealing and appear in every region.
- Leader: **Matriarch Ysolde Vane**, last living Choir singer, kept alive by the Hymn.

### The Lanternfolk (allies)
- A hidden network of escaped Greyborn and sympathetic Hued humans.
- Run the Lantern rest-points across the Pall. Their hub is **Lanternwick**.
- Leader: **Oriel**, an elderly Greyborn who is 40% Husk and hides it under bandages.

### The Hued Remnants (neutral / variable)
- Survivors of the old kingdoms who kept their Hue. They are proud,
  isolationist, and often hostile to Greyborn (whom they see as thieves).
- One enclave per region. The player's Stain colour changes how each enclave
  treats them (see Residue in [03](03-core-gameplay.md)).

### The Husks (tragic enemies)
- Greyborn who held too much. They wander the Pall flooded with a single colour.
- Some are recognisable. Several named NPCs can become Husks if the player
  fails their questlines.

## The six Hues

The Hue wheel is the backbone of combat, art and lore. Adjacent Hues blend;
opposite Hues clash.

```
              CRIMSON
        VIOLET         AMBER
        AZURE          AURUM
              VERDANT
```

| Hue | Element / theme | Virtue (lore) | Vice (Stain) | Opposite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Crimson** | Fire, blood, momentum | Courage | Wrath | Verdant |
| **Amber** | Earth, stone, weight | Endurance | Stubbornness | Azure |
| **Aurum** | Light, sound, judgement | Clarity | Pride | Violet |
| **Verdant** | Growth, rot, poison | Renewal | Hunger | Crimson |
| **Azure** | Water, tide, memory | Calm | Despair | Amber |
| **Violet** | Dusk, illusion, time | Insight | Deceit | Aurum |

**White** is not a Hue. It is all Hues stilled, which means absence of will.
**Grey** is not a Hue either. It is the space that can receive one.

## Tone

Melancholic, reverent, quietly hopeful. Think of a funeral where someone starts
humming. Dialogue is sparse and poetic but never purple. Humour exists in
small, human moments (Lanternfolk banter, an Amber merchant who refuses to
haggle on principle).
