# 38 — Memory Catalogue

Every **Memory** (wordless lore vision, [13](13-legends.md#how-players-learn-the-history-memories))
planned so far, grouped by **Codex page**. Each one is 5–8 seconds of image and
sound, with **no words**. All are proposals.

**Columns:** *Site* is where it's found (map, or event). *Vision* is what the
player sees and hears.

## Page 1 · The Age of Growing (before the Fall)

| # | Memory | Site | Vision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First Light** | Breathing Canopy: oldest tree | The planet's surface, empty and grey. A single root breaks the soil and opens a leaf |
| 2 | **The Herds Begin** | Ashfall Crossing: the north ridge | A single grazer becomes a herd, then a river of herds across the steppe |
| 3 | **The Six Sleeping** | Elder Ribs: the ribcage floor | Six shapes curled in the earth like seeds, waiting |
| 4 | **The Seventh** | The Sleeper: the lake shore | A seventh shape grows, then the earth gently covers it with ice. A heartbeat slows |
| 5 | **The Stillsong** | Shard Reef: the cliff edge | A great whale sings, and the sea goes still for the first time |
