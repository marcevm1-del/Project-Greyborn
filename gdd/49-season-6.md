# 49 — Season 6: The Roaring Comes

**Decided (2026-10-02):** the Clamor arrives in full force, and the planet and
the Murmur must decide whether to stand together. Everything below is a
proposal. **The mirror rule holds** in PvP; the alliance plays out in co-op
modes and community goals.

## How the season feels

- **Both minds are afraid.** The Murmur recognises the Roaring; it has seen
  this before. Hushed Kith stop humming. The planet, half-asleep in Rime, stirs badly.
- **The sky is full of red.** Shards streak down along the red line every night.
- **The question becomes real:** Wildborn and Blightborn have fought for ages.
  Can they stand together for long enough to survive?

## The reveal: why Stillheart sleeps

Season 2 showed the planet growing a seventh answer and putting it to sleep,
**flinching as if afraid** (Memory #25, [38](38-memory-catalogue.md)). Season 6
answers why. **Decided (2026-10-02):**

- Long before the Fall, the planet **felt** something far away in the dark:
  a hunger, roaring between the stars. It grew an answer for it: **cold,
  slow, patient**, the exact opposite of burning hunger. That answer was Stillheart.
- But there was no enemy yet, and the answer frightened the planet: a creature
  made to stop fire could also stop *life*. So it put Stillheart to sleep.
- **The planet wasn't afraid of Stillheart. It was afraid of what it was for.**

**In the game:** during Season 6, Stillheart gains a **Truce-mode passive**,
**Frost Against Fire**: Scald can't spread within 10 m of a Stillheart, and
Clamor creatures nearby are slowed by 20%. *(Co-op only, so PvP balance is untouched.)*

## The Truce, expanded

The Truce mode (two Wildborn and two Blightborn on one team against the
Clamor) becomes the season's featured mode.

| Addition | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Truce Synergies** | When a true pair is split across sides (e.g. a Wildborn Titan and a Blightborn Brawler), their Synergy works as normal and also gives both a **shared shield** for 3 s. Standing together is literally stronger |
| **Mixed territory** | Roots and Blight both count as "held ground". Where they touch, they form **braided ground**: root and crystal woven together. It resists Scald 50% better than either alone |
| **Escalating waves** | Waves build to a **Roar-vent mountain** erupting in the map's centre |
| **The Roar** | The Clamor apex ([37](37-the-clamor.md#the-roar-clamor-apex)) arrives in the final wave |
| **New map** | **Shard Reef, burning**: the coastal map with Scald across the sea and red rain |

**Braided ground** is the season's central image: the planet's roots and the
Murmur's crystal growing into each other to hold back a common enemy.

## PvP during the season

The 4v4 war continues; the mirror is unchanged.
- **Landfalls** ([22](22-season-3.md#clamor-landfall-map-event)) happen **twice per match**.
- **The Roar** can appear in Phase 4 of PvP matches (as in [37](37-the-clamor.md)), attacking both teams.
- **New shared PvP goal:** destroying Landfall cores and damaging the Roar
  earns points for a **shared** community bar (see below).

## Community goals: one shared bar

For the first time, the War Map has a **shared goal** ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)):
- **Hold the Line:** every Clamor creature killed, every Landfall destroyed,
  every Truce match won, by **either side**, fills one shared bar.
- Separate Wildborn and Blightborn front goals continue on the other fronts,
  but the Shattered Coast front is **shared** this season.
- **Lore:** the first time the two sides' efforts count toward the same thing.

## Finale: The Roar Falls

- **Mode:** a special, large Truce event on **Shard Reef, burning**. Mixed
  teams of four fight through escalating Clamor waves to reach and break the
  **Roar-vent mountain**, then face the Roar.
- **Community result**, from the shared bar plus finale results:
  - **The Roaring is held back:** the Clamor's storm breaks. Scald burns out
    to ash across the War Map, and the land begins to recover. A new Memory:
    roots and crystal braided together on a quiet shore. The question for
    Season 7: **does the truce last, now the danger has passed?**
  - **The Roaring takes hold:** the Clamor claims the Shattered Coast as a
    permanent Scald region. The danger stays, and the two minds have to keep
    working together, at least part of the time.
- **Both outcomes** unlock a **braided cosmetic set** (root and crystal
  together) for every player, a first for the game.

## What Season 6 changes for good

1. **Braided ground** exists in the world now: proof the planet and the Murmur can grow together.
2. **Stillheart's purpose** is known: the planet's answer to the Clamor.
3. **The two sides have fought for something together.** The PvP war continues,
   but its meaning has changed.

## How Season 6 feels

Season 6 is the most dramatic season yet. The sky is red every night, the
planet is asleep and vulnerable, and the Clamor falls in force. For the first
time, players see the Murmur afraid: its creatures flinching from the
red streaks, its whispers falling silent.

The emotional centre is the Truce. Players who have fought the other side
for a year and a half are put on mixed teams. The first time a Wildborn Titan
and a Blightborn Brawler land a Smash & Roll together against a Scald Hulk,
and braided ground spreads under their feet, it should feel like something
historic has happened. The Truce is meant to be the moment players remember
when they think of Greyborn's story.

## Braided ground, described

Where roots and crystal meet in a Truce match, they don't fight. The roots
grow into cracks in the crystal; the crystal grows around the roots like
frost around grass. The result is a woven surface, warm amber and cold cyan
threads together, glowing softly. It hums with both sounds at once: a creak
of wood and a chime of glass, in harmony. Scald struggles to take it.

Braided ground is the season's central visual, and it should be beautiful.
It's the image on the key art, the cosmetic set, the loading screens.

## Why Season 6 matters for what follows

Season 6 gives the players something precious so that Season 7 can take it
away ([51](51-season-7.md)). The truce breaks, the braids burn, and the
grief only works because Season 6 made the alliance feel real. The Last
Braid only matters because players remember what braided ground looked
like, in their own matches, under their own feet.
