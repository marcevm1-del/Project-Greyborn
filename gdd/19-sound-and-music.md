# 19 — Sound & Music of Greyborn

Greyborn has **no words**: no speech, no writing (decided). Sound carries
much of the storytelling, and the source pages make **sound signatures** a
gameplay system. This chapter sets the audio identity. All content is a proposal.

## The two voices of the world

| | The planet (Wildborn) | The Murmur (Blightborn) |
|---|---|---|
| Core sound | **Breath, wood and stone**: creaks, low rumbles, wind through leaves, heartbeats deep underground | **Glass and whisper**: chimes, ringing tones, layered whispering that's slightly out of sync |
| Musical palette | Low strings, frame drums, wooden flutes, throat-singing drones, bowed bass, rain sticks | Glass harmonica, bowed metal, crystal bowls, choirs reversed and pitch-shifted, granular textures |
| Rhythm | Slow and organic, like breathing (the Rootwilds' 90 s cycle) | Precise and synchronised, like a single will |
| Silence means… | Rest and safety | Danger: where the Murmur is strongest, nature goes quiet ([15](15-ecology.md#the-murmur-breaks-the-food-web)) |

**Rule:** a player with their eyes closed should be able to tell whether
they're in Wildborn or Blightborn territory.

## Gameplay sound (must be readable)

These are **information**, not decoration, and they have mixing priority.

| Sound | Information it carries |
|---|---|
| **Lineage sound signature** | Which lineage is near, and how big. Louder and deeper with each Stage ([03](03-evolution-system.md)) |
| **Carry signature** | A player carrying 150+ cores has an audible aura; 300+ is louder and on the enemy minimap |
| **Stage transformation** | A map-wide tremor (Wildborn) or ringing chime with whispers (Blightborn), which also tells you the direction |
| **Weak point break** | A sharp crack (Wildborn) or shatter (Blightborn) |
| **Synergy triggers** | Each of the three pair Synergies has a unique, short sting, so both teams know it happened |
| **Node captured / lost** | A low root-pulse (yours) or a dissonant chord (theirs) |
| **Tension at 75+** | A rising drone in that region, warning that a global event is near |
| **Patron event** | **Planet Pulse:** a huge deep exhale from under the ground. **Murmur Surge:** a sudden wall of whispering voices |
| **Wildlife alarms** | Strider Crane cries, herd stampedes, Thornwasp buzz: readable signals of nearby fighting |

**Accessibility:** every gameplay sound has an optional **visual indicator**
(directional ring on screen), so deaf and hard-of-hearing players get the same information.

## Region soundscapes

| Region | Ambient bed | Signature sound |
|---|---|---|
| Heartwood | A deep, slow heartbeat under everything | The First Root humming |
| Rootwilds | Canopy breathing in and out, birdsong, insects | The breath of the canopy rising |
| Underroot | Dripping, distant pulses through the walls | Nerve-light pulses as soft clicks |
| Ashen Steppe | Wind, grass, distant herds | The Grey Migration's rolling thunder |
| Hollow Mire | Muffled, damp, close | Sudden silence when a Hush Cicada colony is near |
| Spirecliffs | Gusting wind, echo | Rams clashing horns far away |
| Bone Flats | Dry wind over bones, a faint whistling | Bones rattling as Harvestmen carry them |
| Rimewastes | Creaking ice, near-silence | A very slow heartbeat under the ice: the Sleeper |
| Cinderveil | Rumbling, hissing, crackle | The Fevermouth's eruption warning |
| Shattered Coast | Calm sea, singing shards | The Shard Reef singing in the wind |
| Glasswaste | **No nature at all**: whispering, ringing glass | The Frozen Herd chiming as the wind passes through it |
| Starwound | A single pulse, like a heartbeat that isn't the planet's | The Seed |

## Music

- **Adaptive by match phase** ([06](06-match-flow.md)):
  1. *Stable Flow:* ambient, sparse, mostly region soundscape.
  2. *Pre-Aggro Resource Control:* rhythm enters (drums or chimes, depending on side).
  3. *Resource Stage:* full ensemble during fights; drops back between them.
  4. *Hunt:* the two musical palettes **collide**: organic and glass themes
     playing against each other.
- **Side-aware:** the player hears their own side's palette in the lead and
  the enemy's as a threat underneath.
- **Main theme:** one melody played two ways: on wooden flute and low strings
  (the planet), and on glass harmonica with whispering voices (the Murmur).
  The Murmur's version is the same tune, *copied and slightly wrong*, which
  matches its nature in the lore.
