# The Heartwood

![[region-heartwood.jpg]]

| Field | Detail |

## From the atlas

A forest of trees so old and vast that their roots are hills. SAP pools
openly in amber lakes. The air hums with the planet's awareness, and
creatures here act strangely calm, as if being watched over.
- **Hazards:** the grasping ground (roots that snare intruders).
- **Wildlife:** Mossback Grazers, Sapback Aphids, Rootwalkers. No infected creatures can survive here for long.
- **Landmark:** **The First Root**, a single root as wide as a mountain, said to reach the planet's core.
- **Match use:** each match map holds a *Heartwood grove*, a fragment of this power (see [10](10-world.md#crater-zones-and-heartwood-groves)).
