# Part 13 — Bestiary Gallery

Concept art for every creature in Parts 3–6: 54 natives, 4 sea creatures, the 7 Clamor
creatures and the 6 Hushed Answers (71 images). Blighted and regional variants are not
drawn yet; their look rules are in §6.2.

These are **AI-generated concept references**, not final art (see §12.14). Where an image
disagrees with a stat block (leg count, size, colour), **the stat block wins**. The
*Tool* column says which model made it: **Gemini** = Google Gemini 3.1 Flash Image via Figma;
**Canva** = Canva's image generator. Prompts are in `tools/art_prompts.py`.
