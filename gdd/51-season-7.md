# 51 — Season 7: The Unbraiding

**Decided (2026-10-02):** the truce breaks. The danger passes, old wounds
reopen, and the war returns harder, with both sides knowing they could have
chosen otherwise. Everything below is a proposal. **The mirror rule holds.**

| **Length** | 10 weeks |
| **Story** | In its Fever, the planet burns out the last of the Scald, and in doing so it also burns the braided ground and the Glass Gardens. The Murmur, burned, falls back into what it knows: taking. The truce comes apart |

## Season 6 carries forward

- **If the Roaring was held back:** Scald has burned out. The danger seems over, and the truce's reason to exist seems to be over too.
- **If the Roaring took the coast:** a Scald region remains. The truce still breaks, but the Clamor is still there, waiting, and both sides know it. The tension is worse.

## How the truce breaks: a tragedy, not a betrayal

Neither mind is the villain ([24](24-the-three-minds.md#rules-for-writers-and-designers)).

1. **The planet's Fever** ([29](29-the-planets-year.md)) rises. In fever, the
   planet burns anything foreign to it. It can't tell Scald from the Murmur's crystal.
2. **The fire takes the braided ground**, the roots and crystal grown
   together in the Truce. The Glass Gardens crack in the heat.
3. **The Murmur feels it as betrayal.** It has been burned once before, on the
   Quiet World ([48](48-beyond-the-sky.md)). It stops creating, stops humming,
   and goes back to the only thing that kept it alive: **taking**.
4. **The planet wakes from its fever** to find the braided ground gone and the
   Murmur advancing again. It doesn't fully understand what it did.

Memories this season show **both sides** of each moment, so players see the
fever from the planet's view and the burning from the Murmur's.

## The world changes

- **Hushed Kith stop humming.** The whispers return to the old,
  synchronised, wordless sound, colder than before.
- **Braided ground unravels.** Where roots and crystal were woven together,
  they pull apart. It's visible on maps as cracked, half-separated growth.
- **The Glass Gardens** that survived become **fortified**: crystal walls grow around them.
- **Wildlife is unsettled.** Creatures that grew used to braided ground are
  restless; overhunting stress ([15](15-ecology.md#overhunting)) triggers more easily this season.

## The Last Braid

Not everything is lost. Somewhere on the Shattered Coast, **one small
patch of braided ground survives the fever**: roots and crystal still woven
together, untouched by either side.

- It appears on the **War Map** as a single, unique point that can't be captured.
- On coastal maps, it can be seen in the distance.
- **Neither mind touches it.** Why is a mystery the story keeps open. It is the
  seed of hope for whatever comes after.

## Gameplay

### PvP: the war returns harder
- The mirror is unchanged. The season's flavour comes from the maps and events:
  - **Braided nodes:** on each map, 2 nodes start as **unravelling braided
    ground**. They're worth **double** Territorial Influence, because both
    sides want to claim what they once shared.
  - **Fever weather** is weighted heavily this season ([39](39-world-events.md)).

### Truce mode becomes "Memory of the Truce"
- The co-op mode stays, but it's now framed as a **Memory**: a vision of Season 6, replayed.
- Its rules are unchanged. Players can still play it, but in the story, the truce is over.
- Completing it unlocks Memories that show what was lost.

### Community goals
- The **shared bar** from Season 6 ([49](49-season-6.md#community-goals-one-shared-bar)) is gone. The War Map returns to **two sides racing**.
- **Braided regions become fronts:** each region that had braided ground in
  Season 6 is now contested. Wildborn goals: purge crystal from the braid.
  Blightborn goals: consume roots from the braid.

## Finale: The Unbraiding

- **Mode:** 4v4 on a special version of **Shard Reef**, where every node starts as unravelling braided ground.
- **Objective:** capture braided nodes to claim them for your side. Each one you
  hold **unravels** toward your material over time.
- **The Last Braid** is visible at the edge of the map all match, untouched.
- **Community result:**
  - **Wildborn unravel more:** the planet's roots reclaim the braided regions.
    The planet is victorious, and quietly, a Memory shows it **missing** the crystal.
  - **Blightborn unravel more:** the Murmur's crystal reclaims them. The
    Murmur is victorious, and quietly, a Memory shows it **humming once**,
    alone, then stopping.
  - Either way, **the Last Braid remains.**

## Where this leaves the story

The war is back, and harder. But both minds have now:
- **fought side by side** (Season 6),
- **created** (the Murmur in Season 5, the planet in answer),
- and **lost something together** (Season 7).

They know what they could be to each other. The Last Braid says it isn't
over. The next direction is the director's call ([50](50-master-timeline.md#threads-still-open)).

## How Season 7 feels

Season 6 ended with the two sides standing together. Season 7 begins with
that memory still fresh, and then takes it away.

In the first weeks, players still see braided ground on every map, and some
still try to fight around it carefully, as if it matters. Then the Fever
comes. The braids catch fire. Players watch roots and crystal, grown together
only weeks ago, burn and pull apart in their matches. The Hushed Kith stop
humming, and the Blightborn's calls turn cold.

It should feel like **grief**. Not anger at the other side, but sadness at
what was lost, and at how easily it happened. The Memories make sure players
see both sides of it: the planet's fever, which it couldn't control, and the
Murmur's terror of being burned again.

Then, in week 7, players discover the Last Braid, and the season's grief gets
a single point of light.

## Writing the tragedy: guidance

1. **Never blame either mind.** The planet's fever is like an illness; the Murmur's
   retreat is like fear. Neither is a choice to betray.
2. **Show loss, not victory.** Even the side that "wins" the finale should feel it lost something.
3. **Keep the Last Braid quiet.** No fanfare, no reward ceremony. It's a small thing that endures.
4. **Leave the door open.** The season ends sad but not hopeless; the Last Braid
   is the promise that the story isn't over.

## A moment in an ordinary match

Breathing Canopy, Season 7. In the centre glade there's a patch of braided
ground left from the Truce, half burnt, half glazed, the threads pulling
apart. A Wildborn Verdant reaches it and starts to root. A Blightborn Hollow
arrives from the other side. Both stop for a second. Their players remember
standing on ground like this together. Then the Hollow opens a rift, and the
Verdant's roots tear through the crystal, and the braid unravels a little
more under them both.

## The finale, walked through

**0:00–4:00.** Every node on the special Shard Reef starts as unravelling

unravels toward its owner's material.

**4:00–8:00.** Fights over the central causeways. The Last Braid glows at the

## The Fever Turn in this season

The Fever is the cause of the tragedy, so it's everywhere:

- **Heat vents and lava** are more active on maps that have them.
- **Predators are aggressive,** making every map feel dangerous.
- **The planet's patron events** fire with more anger: Heartquakes crack the ground harder.
- **The sky** is red at every dusk.

It's important that the Fever is shown as **illness**, not choice. The planet
isn't deciding to destroy the braid; it's burning in a fever it can't control.

## The season's legacy

Season 7 leaves the world sadder, but not empty:

1. **The truce is over** in story; the Truce mode lives on as a Memory.
2. **Braided ground is gone,** except for the Last Braid.
3. **The hum is silent.**
4. **Both minds carry a regret,** shown in the result Memory.
5. **The Last Braid** is the seed for whatever comes next.

## Writing the Last Braid: what not to do

- **Don't explain it.** No Memory should show why it survived.
- **Don't make it powerful.** It grants no bonuses, no buffs, no rewards in matches.
- **Don't make it a quest.** Players can't do anything to it. They can only see it.
- **Don't let it disappear.** Whatever happens next, the Last Braid should still be on that beach.

Its power is that it exists, untouched, in a world that burned everything else.

## For Season 8 and beyond

The Last Braid opens the next chapter of the story, whichever direction the
director chooses ([50](50-master-timeline.md#possible-directions-after-season-7)):
a braid that begins to grow, an Old One who carries both worlds, a descent
into the Seed, or a new answer from the planet. Season 7 ends sad, but it
leaves the door open.

## Closing note

Season 7 is the hardest season to write, and the most important to get right.
It must make players grieve without making them blame anyone, and it must end
on one small, quiet image that makes them hope.

The truce breaks; the braid remains.

Grief, and one woven patch of hope.

The braid remains.
