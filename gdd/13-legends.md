# 13 — Legends of Greyborn

There are no storytellers on Greyborn: no cultures, no writing, no voices
(decided). **The planet itself remembers.** Its history is held in fossils,
landscapes and the instincts of its creatures, and players discover it as
**Memories**. All content in this chapter is a proposal.

## How players learn the history: Memories

- Every map has 2–3 **Memory sites**: a fossil, a strange root formation, or a
  scar in the land.
- Touching one outside combat (2 s channel) plays a **wordless 5–8 second
  vision** of the planet remembering. It's in sound and image only, in keeping
  with the no-words rule.
- Each Memory is added to a **Memory Codex** across matches. It gives no power,
  only lore and cosmetic unlocks (e.g. a Memory-themed skin border).
- Memories are written so they work in any order. Together they tell the story below.
- The full list of 40 planned Memories is in [38 — Memory Catalogue](38-memory-catalogue.md).

## The First Answers

Long before the Fall, the planet grew **one creature of each lineage** to see
what it could become. These were the **First Answers**, the original Titan,
Brawler, Verdant, Hollow, Thornrunner and Bonespire. They had no names, only
the shapes the land remembers them by.

| Lineage | First Answer | What became of it | Where it rests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Titan | **The Mountain That Walked** | Lay down at the end of the Fall and became a mountain range | **The Vertebrae** (Spirecliffs): the peaks are its spine |
| Brawler | **The Breaker** | Held the shoreline against the first Blightborn tide. The ground broke under its fists | **Shattered Coast**: the cliffs are the cracks it made |
| Verdant | **The Breath** | Grew an entire forest as a living wall to slow the Blight | **The Breathing Canopy** (Rootwilds): the forest still breathes with its rhythm |
| Hollow | **The Quiet** | Sank into the earth to seal the Underroot against the Murmur's whispers | **The Swallowing Pool** (Hollow Mire): its silence still fills the swamp |
| Thornrunner | **The Hunt That Never Ended** | Never died. It still runs | Seen at a distance on the **Ashen Steppe**. The Grey Migration follows it |
| Bonespire | **The Rememberer** | Gathered the bones of every creature lost in the Fall so the planet would not forget them | **The Elder Ribs** (Bone Flats): its own ribcage, around the fossils it kept |

*(Earlier docs said the Elder Ribs "may have belonged to the first Titan". In
legend, creatures believe that, but the Memory sites reveal it was the Rememberer.)*

### The Sleeper: a seventh answer?

Under the ice of the **Rimewastes** lies the **Sleeper**, a creature that matches
**none** of the six lineages. The Memories hint at an explanation: before the
six, the planet tried a **seventh answer** and stopped. Whether it failed,
was too dangerous, or is being *saved* is unknown.

**Design hook:** the Sleeper is a natural route to a **seventh lineage** for
the roster (open question 13), or the focus of a future seasonal event. It is
left unresolved on purpose.

## The Fall, as the Memories tell it

1. **The Sky Breaks.** A light crosses the northern sky. The impact shakes the
   whole Greyreach. Every creature on the planet feels pain at the same
   moment. *(Memory site: the Shard Reef.)*
2. **The First Whisper.** The Murmur seeps from the Starwound. Wildlife at the
   crater's edge falls silent, then turns, all at once, in perfect step.
   *(Memory site: the Frozen Herd, Glasswaste.)*
3. **The First Answers Rise.** The planet wakes the six. The Breaker holds the
   coast. The Breath grows its forest-wall. The Quiet descends to seal the
   deep places. *(Memory sites: the Shattered Coast cliffs, the Breathing Canopy.)*
4. **The Hushed.** The Murmur studies the six as they fight and **copies
   them**. The first corrupted Ascendants appear: glass versions of the
   planet's own answers. *(Memory site: a glass fossil in the Bone Flats.)*
5. **The Long Stand.** The First Answers hold the Murmur to the north for an
   age. One by one they fall, or lie down, or become the land.
6. **The Whispering.** With the First Answers gone, the planet sleeps to heal.
   The Murmur spreads slowly through the Underroot and the coasts.
7. **The Answering.** The planet wakes again and begins calling broods. The
   present war, and every match, begins.

## The Hushed Answers

When the Murmur copied the First Answers, it made **six glass mirrors** of them:
the **Hushed Answers**. They didn't fall. They wait at the edge of the
**Starwound**, perfect and silent.

| Hushed Answer | Copy of | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **The Glass Range** | The Mountain That Walked | A walking mountain of black crystal that hums at a pitch you feel in your teeth |
| **The Shatterer** | The Breaker | Every blow sends out a ring of glass shards |
| **The Lattice Grove** | The Breath | A forest of crystal trees that slowly walk |
| **The Unspoken** | The Quiet | Can't be seen, only heard: whispers moving through the air |
| **The Endless Pursuit** | The Hunt That Never Ended | Runs in the opposite direction around the world, always just out of sight of its original |
| **The Archive** | The Rememberer | Keeps glass copies of every creature the Murmur has taken |

Each Hushed Answer is designed as a full encounter in [40](40-hushed-answers.md).

**Design hooks:** the Hushed Answers are natural candidates for **seasonal
boss events**, **Starwound endgame encounters**, or a **PvE mode** (all
proposals). The pairing *First Answer ↔ Hushed Answer* gives each lineage a
legendary rival.

## Myths creatures act out

There are no words, but creatures behave as if they remember. These
behaviours make the legends visible in the world:

- **Strays face north at dusk.** Wild Base Forms all turn toward the
  Starwound when the sun sets, and stand still for a few seconds.
- **The Grey Migration** always moves the same way the Hunt That Never Ended is
  running.
- **Bonewrights** build their totems facing the Elder Ribs.
- **Blighted creatures** pause when they pass a Memory site, as if listening.
  The Murmur is learning the planet's history too.

## The First Answers, in portrait

**The Mountain That Walked.** The first Titan was not a warrior. It was a
shaper: it walked, and where it walked, mountains rose behind it. In the
Fall, it turned its strength against the glass tide for the first time. When
the war ended for it, it lay down along the Spirecliffs, and its spine became
the Vertebrae. On clear days, the shape of its head can still be seen in the
northernmost peak.

**The Breaker.** The first Brawler was the planet's storm: restless, loud,
always moving. When the first Blightborn tide poured down the coast, it stood
on the shore and hammered the ground until the cliffs cracked into the sea,
breaking the tide's path. The fist-shaped craters down the cliffs are its
work ([41](41-landmarks.md)).

**The Breath.** The first Verdant breathed, and forests grew. In the Fall, it
grew a wall of trees so dense the Murmur couldn't pass, and then it became
part of that wall. The Rootwilds still breathe in its rhythm.

**The Quiet.** The first Hollow held the deep places. When the Murmur's
whispers reached the Underroot, it sank down and sealed the nerves of the
planet with its own silence. The swamp above still holds that silence.

**The Hunt That Never Ended.** The first Thornrunner chose never to stop. It
still runs, around and around the world, and the herds follow it. Some say
it's still hunting the first Blightborn that escaped it.

**The Rememberer.** The first Bonespire gathered the bones of everything
lost in the Fall, so the planet would never forget. Its ribcage, the Elder
Ribs, still shelters those bones.

## Telling legends without words

Because the world has no words, every legend is told through four layers:

1. **The landscape:** the Vertebrae, the cracked cliffs, the breathing forest.
2. **Creature behaviour:** the herds following the Hunt; Strays facing north.
3. **Memories:** short wordless visions at map sites ([38](38-memory-catalogue.md)).
4. **Music:** each First Answer has a short motif, woven into its region's soundscape.

A player who never opens the Codex should still sense the legends from the
world around them. A player who does will find them told in full.
