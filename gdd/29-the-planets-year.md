# 29 — The Planet's Year

Greyborn is alive, and like any living thing it has **cycles**. This chapter
defines the planet's natural year, called **Turns** so it isn't confused with
the live-service seasons ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)). All content is a proposal.

## The four Turns

The planet's year has four Turns, like breaths or moods. Each lasts about a
quarter of the year.

| Turn | The planet is… | The world | The Murmur |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bloom** | Growing | Sap rises, flowers open, herds calve, Strays bud in large numbers | Blight grows slowly. Fresh life is harder to take |
| **Ash** | Shedding | Leaves fall grey, ash drifts from the Cinderveil, the Grey Migration crosses the Steppe | Normal pace |
| **Rime** | Sleeping | Frost spreads from the Rimewastes, creatures slow, the Sleeper's heartbeat grows clearer | Blight spreads **faster**. The planet's defences are lowest while it sleeps |
| **Fever** | Fighting | Volcanoes stir, storms build, predators grow bolder | Blight cracks in the heat. The planet's counter-attack |

**Lore:** the Murmur gains most during **Rime**, when the planet sleeps. The
planet hits back in **Fever**. The war has a rhythm the whole world feels.

## How Turns show up in the game

Each live season ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)) happens in one Turn of the planet's year:

| Live season | Turn | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Season 1: The Hush Wakes | **Ash** | The Migration, ash on the steppe, a glass mountain on the horizon |
| Season 2: The Sleeper Stirs | **Rime** | Frost, sleep, the Sleeper's heartbeat |
| Season 3: The Second Light | **Fever** | Storms and burning, the Clamor's arrival |
| Season 4 | **Bloom** | A season of renewal (story open) |

### What a Turn changes in matches

Only **atmosphere, weather and wildlife**. Never the balance between sides.

| Turn | Weather pool weighted toward | Wildlife | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bloom | Sap Rain, Clear | More grazers and calves; Strays bud more often | Flowers, fresh green, brighter light |
| Ash | Ashfall, Clear | Migrations, Ember Moths | Grey-gold light, drifting ash |
| Rime | Rime Fog, Breathing Night | Rimewastes variants on all maps; creatures slower to respawn | Frost on every surface, cold light |
| Fever | Shard Storm, Ashfall | Predators more aggressive; Cinderveil variants appear | Heat haze, red sky at dusk |

**Fairness note:** the Rime and Fever lore says one side has the upper hand,
but **in matches both sides are equal**. Turns only colour the story told on
the War Map ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)): for example, Blightborn community
goals could be framed as "spread during the planet's sleep" in Rime, without
changing the target.

## The planet's day

| Time | The world |
|---|---|
| **Dawn** | The canopy exhales mist. Grazers move to the open |
| **Day** | The Grey Eye's soft light. Herds graze; predators rest |
| **Dusk** | Strays face north (toward the Starwound). Moth blooms rise |
| **Night** | The Lantern moon and the Fall-line's glow. Predators hunt; Lanternmoss and Blight light the ground |

Matches roll a time of day ([12](12-life-on-greyborn.md#weather-and-time-of-day)).
Dusk and Night are the most atmospheric and should come up often in the
weighting.

## The Turns in depth

### Bloom: growing

The planet wakes from Rime with something like joy. Sap rises so fast that
bark splits and bleeds amber. Flowers open across the Rootwilds in waves that
can be seen from the Spirecliffs. The Mossback herds calve, and for a few
weeks the steppe is full of small, unsteady legs. Strays bud in great numbers,
and wild Kith broods play in every clearing.

The sound of Bloom is busy: birdsong, buzzing Lumen Bees, the creak of growth.
The light is brighter than at any other time of year, though still softened by
the planet's haze. In matches, Bloom feels generous: more healing plants, more
wildlife, more SAP. It's the Turn in which the planet is strongest at
creating, which is why Season 5's turning point, when the Murmur learns to
create too, lands so hard in the Ash that follows.

The Murmur dislikes Bloom. Fresh life is harder to take: it fights back with
growth. Blight spreads slowly, and Glass Gardens look strange and out of
place among so much colour.

### Ash: shedding

The planet lets go. Leaves fall grey and drift for days. Ash blows from the
Cinderveil and settles on everything, so the world becomes the softest
version of its own name. The Grey Migration crosses the Ashen Steppe in a
river of herds that takes weeks to pass.

Ash is a Turn of movement and memory. Creatures travel; Strays face north at
dusk for longer; Memory sites seem to glow more brightly. Sound carries far in
the dry air, so sound signatures matter more. In matches, Ash means long
sightlines broken by drifting ash, and the constant presence of herds.

### Rime: sleeping

Frost spreads from the Rimewastes until it reaches every region. The planet
sleeps, and its heartbeat slows so far that only the Sleeper's can be heard
beneath the ice. Creatures move slowly; some hibernate in burrows and caves.
The light is cold and low.

Rime is the Murmur's season. While the planet sleeps, Blight creeps fastest,
and the whispers grow bolder. It's no accident that Season 2 (the Sleeper
waking) and Season 6 (the Clamor's Roaring) both fall in Rime: it's the time
of the planet's greatest weakness and its deepest secrets.

### Fever: fighting

The planet wakes angry. Volcanoes stir across the Cinderveil, storms build
over the Stillsea, and predators grow bold. The air shimmers with heat, and
the sky turns red at every dusk. The planet is trying to burn the infection
out of its body.

Fever is dangerous for everyone. Blight cracks in the heat, but so does
everything else. Season 7's tragedy happens in Fever: the planet, burning
everything foreign in its sickness, destroys the braided ground it built with
the Murmur ([51](51-season-7.md)). In matches, Fever brings heat vents,
aggressive wildlife and Embergrass fires.

## Between the Turns

The change from one Turn to the next isn't instant. Each transition lasts
about a week of in-game atmosphere:

| Transition | What players see |
|---|---|
| Bloom → Ash | Leaves turn, the first ash drifts in, calves grow into yearlings |
| Ash → Rime | Frost at dawn, herds thinning, the first fog |
| Rime → Fever | Ice cracking, steam rising, the first rumbles from the volcanoes |
| Fever → Bloom | Rain after the storms, green shoots through burnt ground |

## The Turns and the creatures

| Creature | Bloom | Ash | Rime | Fever |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mossback Grazers | Calving | Migrating | Sheltering in valleys | Restless |
| Ember Moths | Few | Great dusk blooms | Absent | Swarming the volcanoes |
| Strays | Budding in great numbers | Facing north for longer | Huddled in broods | Fleeing the heat |
| Predators | Hunting calves | Following herds | Hungry, bold | Aggressive |
| Blighted creatures | Uneasy | Normal | Bold | Sluggish in the heat |

## Why the planet has a year at all

Greyborn is a living thing, and living things have rhythms. The Turns make the
planet feel like a body rather than a backdrop: one that grows, rests, sleeps
and fights. They also give the live game a natural calendar. Players come to
expect the Migration in Ash and the Long Cold in Rime the way they expect real
seasons, and each season's story feels rooted in the time of year it happens.
