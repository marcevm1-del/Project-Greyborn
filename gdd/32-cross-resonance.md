# 32 — Cross-Resonance (the other 12 pairings)

The source pages define **three pair Synergies** (Titan + Brawler, Verdant +
Hollow, Thornrunner + Bonespire; see [04](04-synergies.md)). The six launch
lineages make **15 possible pairs**. [04](04-synergies.md#open-the-other-12-pairings)
proposed small **cross-resonance** passives for the other 12. This chapter
designs them. All are proposals.

## Rules

- A cross-resonance is a **small passive**, active while the two Ascendants
  are within **15 m** of each other. No new button, no Synergy ability.
- It's always **weaker than a true pair Synergy**. The three pairs remain the
  strongest choice; cross-resonance makes **mixed teams** viable and interesting.
- Each one is a **flavour** of how those two parts of the planet's body work together.
- Unlocks at **Level 8** for both partners (before the first true Synergy at L12).
- **Stillheart** works differently. It's a universal partner with Attunement ([21](21-season-2.md#pairing-universal-partner-decided)).

## The 12 cross-resonances

| Pair | Name | Effect | The planet's idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Titan + Verdant** | **Rooted Mountain** | Titan's Bulwark also protects Verdant-rooted nodes behind it from being uprooted | Stone shelters growth |
| **Titan + Hollow** | **Cave and Stone** | Enemies pulled or slowed by Hollow take +15% damage from Quake Slam | The mountain over the deep |
| **Titan + Thornrunner** | **Herd and Hunter** | Thornrunner's Thorn Dash through a Titan-staggered target resets once | The hunter follows the landslide |
| **Titan + Bonespire** | **Ridge and Spine** | Standing behind a Bulwarking Titan, the Bonespire's lances fly 20% farther | The spine and the ribs |
| **Brawler + Verdant** | **Wild Growth** | Brawler kills on a Verdant-rooted node heal the Brawler 10% | Fury feeds the forest |
| **Brawler + Hollow** | **Storm in the Dark** | Roll Commit through a Void Rift is 30% faster | Wind through caves |
| **Brawler + Thornrunner** | **Two Claws** | Hitting the same target within 2 s adds a 1 s bleed | Two predators, one prey |
| **Brawler + Bonespire** | **Break and Mark** | A Brawler's Grapple exposes the target's weak point for 3 s | Breaking open the bones |
| **Verdant + Thornrunner** | **Thicket Hunters** | Thornrunners moving through Verdant territory are hidden from the minimap | Thorns in the undergrowth |
| **Verdant + Bonespire** | **Root and Fossil** | Ossuary walls on rooted ground last 50% longer | The roots grow round old bones |
| **Hollow + Thornrunner** | **Silent Hunt** | Thornrunner's sound signature is muted while within 15 m of the Hollow | Silence for the hunter |
| **Hollow + Bonespire** | **Echo of Bone** | Lances fired into a Void Rift come out the other side of it, with +10% damage | Sound in the deep, memory in the dark |

## Coverage check

Every lineage has **one true pair** (from the source pages) and **four
cross-resonances**, plus Stillheart's Attunement with all of them.

| Lineage | True pair | Cross-resonances with |
|---|---|---|
| Titan | Brawler | Verdant, Hollow, Thornrunner, Bonespire |
| Brawler | Titan | Verdant, Hollow, Thornrunner, Bonespire |
| Verdant | Hollow | Titan, Brawler, Thornrunner, Bonespire |
| Hollow | Verdant | Titan, Brawler, Thornrunner, Bonespire |
| Thornrunner | Bonespire | Titan, Brawler, Verdant, Hollow |
| Bonespire | Thornrunner | Titan, Brawler, Verdant, Hollow |

## What this does to the draft

A team of four can field **at most two true pairs**, plus up to four
cross-resonances among its other combinations. For example, **Titan,
Brawler, Verdant, Hollow** has two true pairs (Commit, Root & Void) and four
cross-resonances (Rooted Mountain, Cave and Stone, Wild Growth, Storm in the
Dark). Drafting becomes a puzzle of **maximising connections**, which is
the source pages' "interdependency" made into team-building.

## What each cross-resonance looks and sounds like

When two Ascendants are within range and their cross-resonance is active, a
faint visual thread connects them: a line of amber pollen (Wildborn) or a
shimmer of glass dust (Blightborn). Each pairing also has its own small effect.

- **Rooted Mountain (Titan + Verdant):** moss spreads from the Verdant's
  footprints up the Titan's legs. Behind Bulwark, rooted nodes glow a little
  brighter, showing they're protected.
- **Cave and Stone (Titan + Hollow):** the Titan's slams make a hollow, echoing
  boom inside the Hollow's rifts, like a stone dropped down a well.
- **Herd and Hunter (Titan + Thornrunner):** dust from the Titan's slam hangs
  in the air, and the Thornrunner's dash cuts a clear trail through it.
- **Ridge and Spine (Titan + Bonespire):** the Bonespire's lances leave a faint
  ridge-shaped trail when fired over the Titan's shoulder.
- **Wild Growth (Brawler + Verdant):** a Brawler's kill on rooted ground makes
  flowers burst up around the body.
- **Storm in the Dark (Brawler + Hollow):** the Brawler's roll through a rift
  roars like wind through a cave.
- **Two Claws (Brawler + Thornrunner):** paired claw marks appear on the target.
- **Break and Mark (Brawler + Bonespire):** the grabbed target's weak point
  cracks visibly, pointing the Bonespire to it.
- **Thicket Hunters (Verdant + Thornrunner):** the Thornrunner's thorns sprout
  leaves while on Verdant territory, blending in.
- **Root and Fossil (Verdant + Bonespire):** roots grow around Ossuary walls,
  making them look like ancient fossils.
- **Silent Hunt (Hollow + Thornrunner):** the Thornrunner's skitter fades to
  nothing; only a faint thrum of the Hollow's silence remains.
- **Echo of Bone (Hollow + Bonespire):** lances vanish into one side of a rift
  and burst out of the other with a doubled crack.

## How players learn cross-resonance

- **In the draft:** when a player hovers a lineage, the draft screen draws
  thin lines to teammates' lineages, solid for a true pair and dotted for a
  cross-resonance, with a short description of each.
- **In the match:** the first time a cross-resonance activates, a small icon
  appears beside both players' portraits with its name.
- **In the Den:** a training drill for each cross-resonance ([27](27-modes.md#the-den-training)).

## Tuning targets

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Win-rate gain from one active cross-resonance | +0.5% to +1.5% |
| Win-rate gain from one active true pair | +3% to +5% |
| Share of matches where a team has at least one cross-resonance | Over 90% (almost every team has some) |
| Share of matches where a team has two true pairs | 30–50% (common, but not required) |

If a cross-resonance pushes past its target, its numbers are cut first, never
the true pair it shares a lineage with.
