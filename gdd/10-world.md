# 10 — The World of Greyborn

> **Decided by the director (2026-10-02):** primal nature fantasy · the planet
> is **alive and aware** · **only creatures**, no cultures or civilisations · a
> **meteorite carrying a disease** struck Greyborn · the disease is a **hive mind** ·
> one team is **nature's response**, the other is the **infected** · both teams play
> the **same six lineages** (the infected side in corrupted forms) · sides are
> **assigned each match** · the infection spreads **Blight** instead of roots,
> **infects wildlife**, leaves **crater zones**, and **nature fights back**.
>
> **Names approved (2026-10-02):** Starwound, the Murmur, Wildborn, Blightborn, Heartwood.
> See also [11 — Atlas](11-atlas.md) and [12 — Life on Greyborn](12-life-on-greyborn.md).

## Greyborn, the living planet

Greyborn is a world of moss-grey stone, ash-grey grassland and endless forest.
It isn't just covered in life: **it is alive**, one enormous organism that is
aware in a slow, deep way.

Its body works like this:

| What players see | What it is to the planet |
|---|---|
| **Roots** spreading over the ground | The planet's nerves, how it feels and claims its surface |
| **Nodes** | Nerve clusters; places where the planet's awareness gathers |
| **SAP** | Its lifeblood, rising from the deep |
| **Resource Hubs** | Its hearts: great knots of roots where SAP pools |
| **Base Forms** | Its newborn creatures, budded from the soil like fruit |
| **Ascendants** | Its *answers*: shapes it grows its creatures into when it needs them |

The planet doesn't speak in words. It communicates through **growth, tremor
and instinct**: roots that reach toward danger, ground that shakes beneath an
intruder, a sudden urge every creature in a valley feels at once.

### The six lineages are the planet's six answers

| Lineage | What the planet expresses through it |
|---|---|
| **Titan** | Its bones: mountains that walk |
| **Brawler** | Its fury: the force of a storm or a landslide |
| **Verdant** | Its growth: forest, healing, spreading roots |
| **Hollow** | Its silence: deep caves, the dark beneath the roots |
| **Thornrunner** | Its hunters: the thorn and the claw |
| **Bonespire** | Its memory: fossils, the bones of everything that came before |

## The Starwound (the meteorite)

Long ago (not in any recorded age, since Greyborn has no one to record), a
burning stone fell from the sky and struck the planet. Its crater is called
the **Starwound**.

The stone wasn't empty. It carried a **disease**: a living infection that
spread out of the crater into the soil, the roots and the creatures.

### The Murmur (the hive mind)

The disease is a **hive mind**, a single will from beyond the stars, called
the **Murmur** after the whispering sound that infected ground makes.

- **It cannot create, only copy and corrupt.** The Murmur has no creatures of
  its own. It infects Greyborn's Base Forms as they bud from blighted soil and
  grows them along the planet's own six lineages, twisted toward its purpose.
  This is the in-world reason both teams share one roster.
- **It spreads Blight.** Where the planet grows roots, the Murmur grows
  **Blight**: glassy, crystalline growth that whispers. Blight is the Murmur's
  nervous system, just as roots are the planet's.
- **It wants the planet's heart.** The Murmur feeds on SAP. Its goal is to
  reach the deep heart of Greyborn and make the whole planet one infected body.
- **It is patient and many-voiced.** Infected creatures act in perfect
  coordination, as if one mind were moving them, because one is.

## The two sides

| | **Wildborn** (nature's response) | **Blightborn** (the infected) |
|---|---|---|
| Who they are | Base Forms the planet grows on purpose to fight the infection, its immune response | Base Forms budded from blighted ground and taken by the Murmur |
| Territory | Spreads **Roots** | Spreads **Blight** |
| Patron | **Greyborn**, the living planet | **The Murmur**, the hive mind |
| Look | Moss, bark, amber sap, bone-white horn | Black glassy crystal, starlit cyan-violet glow, a hollow chitin shine |
| Sound | Creak, growl, wind through leaves | Layered whispering; voices out of sync |
| Lineages | All six | All six, in corrupted forms |

**Sides are assigned every match.** Every player plays both. Gameplay is a
**mirror**: same lineages, same numbers. Differences are visual, audio and
flavour, with only small mechanical touches kept in balance (see below).

## How the lore shows up in a match

### Roots vs. Blight
Both teams capture nodes the same way (see [05](05-territory-and-economy.md));
only the material differs. Wildborn root nodes; Blightborn **blight** them.
Uprooting an enemy node is **purging** Blight for Wildborn and **consuming**
roots for Blightborn.

### Patron interventions ("nature fights back")
When a border's Tension reaches 100, a global event fires. The event deck is
split by patron:
- **Planet Pulse** events: Greyborn acts. Quakes, healing blooms, wildlife turning on infected players.
- **Murmur Surge** events: the hive mind acts. Shard rain, whispers that reveal positions, Blight erupting from the ground.
- **Neutral** events: the old shared deck (SAP Surge, Core Bloom…).

**Which deck fires:** the patron of the team that is **behind** in
Territorial Influence intervenes; within 5% of each other, it's the neutral
deck. In lore, each patron protects its own children when they're losing. In
gameplay it's a comeback mechanic against snowballing.

### Infected wildlife
Neutral creatures can be **infected** (Blightborn) or **rallied** (Wildborn)
to fight for a team for a while. See [09 — Wildlife](09-wildlife.md#infection-and-rallying).

### Crater zones and Heartwood groves
Every map has **one Starwound crater** and **one Heartwood grove**:
- **Starwound crater:** glassy, blighted ground. Blight spreads **2× faster**
  there and roots 50% slower. Hazard: **shard storms**. Reward: **Starshard
  veins** that release large core caches when broken.
- **Heartwood grove:** an ancient root-knot where the planet is strongest.
  Roots spread 2× faster, Blight 50% slower. Hazard: the **grasping ground**,
  where roots snare intruders. Reward: **SAP springs**.
- The two sites sit on the map's two flanks, each equidistant from both bases.
  Each side has a friendly zone and a hostile one, but neither is closer to
  either team's base (see [16](16-launch-maps.md#rules-every-map-follows)).

## Timeline

| Age | What happened |
|---|---|
| **The Age of Growing** | Greyborn alone: forests, beasts, the six answers already sleeping in its creatures |
| **The Fall** | The Starwound strikes. The planet feels pain for the first time |
| **The Whispering** | The Murmur spreads quietly. Wildlife sickens. The first Blightborn bud from infected soil |
| **The Answering** (present day) | The planet wakes fully and grows its Wildborn to fight back. Every match is one battle in this war |

## Tone guide

- **Primal and majestic, not grimdark.** The planet is beautiful and
  dangerous; the Murmur is unsettling but awe-inspiring, not gory.
- **No words in the world.** No writing, no speech, no cultures. Story is told
  through landscape, creature behaviour, sound, and how the world reacts.
- **Both sides are true to their nature.** The Murmur isn't evil in a human
  sense; it's hunger and will. Playing Blightborn should feel thrilling, not shameful.
