# 45 — The Kith

The Kith are the native humanoid creatures of Greyborn that every player
starts as (decided: native wildlife; name: Kith). Their life cycle is in
[12](12-life-on-greyborn.md#the-kith-life-cycle). This chapter is a full
portrait. All details beyond the decided facts are proposals.

## What a Kith is

- **Size:** about knee-height to an adult stag, slender and quick.
- **Body:** grey skin, moss-flecked, with soft bark-like plates on the
  shoulders and spine. Large amber eyes adapted to dim forest light.
- **Hands:** four long fingers, good for climbing and digging.
- **No speech, no tools, no culture** (decided: only creatures). Kith are animals,
  intelligent the way crows or wolves are, not the way people are.
- **Potential:** every Kith carries all six lineages inside it, sleeping. Most
  never awaken. That's what makes Kith special among Greyborn's creatures.

## Kith behaviour

| Behaviour | Description |
|---|---|
| **Brood bond** | Kith that budded together stay together for life. A separated Kith calls until its brood answers |
| **Foraging** | They eat SAP-rich plants (Sapbloom, Amber Beetles) and small critters |
| **Nesting** | Broods sleep in a ring around their birth-node, facing outward |
| **Dusk ritual** | At sunset, wild Kith turn north toward the Starwound and stand still ([13](13-legends.md#myths-creatures-act-out)). No one knows why. Instinct, or memory |
| **Grieving** | When a brood-mate dies, the others stay with the body until it sinks into the soil |
| **Play** | Young Kith chase each other, wrestle and climb. It's the only time they seem carefree |

These behaviours appear in **Strays** (wild Kith, [09](09-wildlife.md)) in
every match, in onboarding ([25](25-calls-and-onboarding.md)), and as player
emotes ([25](25-calls-and-onboarding.md#instinct-emotes-out-of-combat)).

## Three kinds of Kith

| Kind | Who they are | How they differ |
|---|---|---|
| **Strays** | Wild Kith that were never called | Live and die as small creatures. Can evolve on their own if left near territory long enough ([09](09-wildlife.md)) |
| **Called Kith** (Wildborn) | Broods the planet called to fight | Feel the planet's hunger as their own; begin to evolve |
| **Hushed Kith** (Blightborn) | Broods that budded in blighted soil | Hear the Murmur's whisper from birth. Grey skin glazed with glass; eyes flicker violet |

**Hushed Kith aren't villains.** They were born into the whisper and know
nothing else. For them, the voices are comfort and company ([28](28-tales-and-trailer.md#tale-2--the-whisper)).

## Why the Kith?

A central lore question: **why does the planet call Kith, and not its
other creatures, to become Ascendants?** Proposal:

- The First Answers ([13](13-legends.md#the-first-answers)) were **Kith** that
  the planet grew to full potential. The Kith are the planet's original
  "blank" creature, the one shape that can become any of the six answers.
- That's also why the **Murmur wants Kith most**: copying a Kith gives it
  access to all six lineages at once.
- And it's why **Stillheart** matters: it's the seventh shape *inside* every
  Kith, one the planet chose to keep asleep ([21](21-season-2.md)).

## The Kith life span

| Stage | Wild Stray | Called Kith (a match) |
|---|---|---|
| Budding | Day 1 | Match start |
| Young | First few months | Levels 1–2 |
| Adult | Most of its life | Levels 3–9 (lineage awakens) |
| Elder | Grey-muzzled, slow, still with its brood | **Doesn't apply:** a called Kith evolves instead of aging |
| Death | Sinks into the soil and returns to the planet | Dies in battle and returns to the soil, or reaches Ascendant |

**What happens to Ascendants after the war?** Explored in [46 — Wild Ascendants & the Old Ones](46-wild-ascendants.md). The First Answers
lay down and became landscape ([13](13-legends.md)). Perhaps every Ascendant
eventually does the same.

## Kith in the art and audio

- **Proportions:** big head and eyes, long limbs, small body, so the Kith reads
  as young and vulnerable at Level 1, which makes evolution feel like growth.
- **Sound:** soft chirps, clicks and a low hum when content. A distress call
  that every Kith (and every player) learns to recognise.
- **Silhouette:** must be clearly distinct from every lineage at Stage 1, so
  enemies can tell a Kith in Base Form from an awakened one at a distance.
