# 22 — Season 3: The Second Light

> **Decided (2026-10-02):** the second light on the Fall-line is a **second
> meteorite carrying a rival hive mind**. Names, behaviour and mechanics below are proposals.

## The rival: the Clamor

Where the Murmur is quiet, patient and crystalline, its rival is the opposite.

| | The Murmur | **The Clamor** (proposed name) |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | Whispers, perfectly in sync | **Roaring noise**: a thousand voices shouting over each other |
| Growth | **Blight**: cold glass | **Scald**: hot, rust-red spore-crust that steams and crackles |
| Pace | Never hurries | **Ravenous and fast**. Spreads in bursts, burns itself out, spreads again |
| Method | Copies and corrupts the planet's creatures | **Devours.** It doesn't copy anything. It consumes roots, Blight and creatures alike |
| Goal | Reach the Heartwood and make the planet one infected body | Unknown. It may be chasing the Murmur across the stars |

**The story turn:** the Clamor eats **Blight as hungrily as roots**. For the
first time, the planet and the Murmur share an enemy. Whether that leads to
anything like a truce is a question for later seasons.

## How the Clamor appears in matches

The game stays **4v4, Wildborn vs. Blightborn**, and the mirror rule holds.
The Clamor is a **hostile third force**, like wildlife: it isn't playable and
it attacks both teams.

### Clamor Landfall (map event)
- Once per match in Phase 2 or 3, a burning shard falls along the Fall-line
  and **strikes a random flank of the map** (warning: 30 s, a red streak across the sky).
- The impact creates a **Landfall site**:
  - **Scald** spreads from it, eating root *and* Blight cells at the same rate
    and turning them neutral and uncapturable.
  - **Clamor spawn** creatures pour out and attack whoever is nearest.
- **The Landfall core** at the centre can be destroyed. The team that lands
  the final blow gets a large core cache (300) and stops the Scald spreading.
- **The dilemma:** both teams lose territory to the Scald, so both want it
  stopped, but only one gets the reward. Fight the Clamor first, or each
  other? Let the enemy weaken it, then steal the last hit?

### Clamor creatures (proposed)

| Creature | Body plan | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Shriekers** | Six-legged | Fast swarms that scream. Their noise **reveals all players** within 20 m |
| **Scald Hulks** | Four-legged | Slow, heavily armoured. Leave a burning trail of Scald |
| **Ashmouths** | Two-legged | Devour the corpses of anything nearby to grow stronger |
| **Spore Kites** | Insect | Flying spore-carriers that seed new patches of Scald |

The full Clamor roster, including its apex, **the Roar**, is in [37 — The Clamor in Depth](37-the-clamor.md).

### Wildlife and the Clamor
- Clamor creatures **eat wildlife**. A Landfall near herds drives them into a panic.
- Neither Wildborn nor Blightborn can rally or infect Clamor creatures. They can't be turned.

### Front goals

The Clamor adds a **shared** goal action to every front: **destroying
Landfall cores**. Both sides earn front points for it, so each side's
community has a reason to fight the Clamor.

### Week-by-week

| Week | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | Stillheart launches. The second light is visibly brighter on the Fall-line |
| 2–3 | Red streaks cross the sky. Strays and Blighted creatures **both** turn to watch them |
| 4 | **First Landfall** event goes live on all maps |
| 5 | A Memory unlocks, from the Murmur's side: *the Murmur remembers fleeing something across the dark* |
| 6–7 | Landfalls grow larger. Scald patches appear on the War Map |
| 8 | **Patron week:** Planet Pulse and Murmur Surge events can now also strike Landfall sites |
| 9 | Fronts close |
| 10 | **Finale: The Second Stone** |

### Finale mode: The Second Stone (proposal)

- **Map:** a special version of **Shard Reef**. The second meteorite crashes into the reef at the start of the match.
- **Objective:** the Clamor floods the map from the crash site. Both teams
  fight each other *and* the Clamor. Score comes from **territory you still
  hold at the end** and **Clamor creatures you destroy**.
- **Community result:** if players destroy enough of the Clamor, the second
  stone is **contained** (Season 4 begins with the Clamor weakened). If not, it
  **takes root**, and Season 4 begins with a new Scald-covered region on the War Map.

## Open threads for Season 4 and beyond

- **Does the Murmur fear the Clamor?** The Season 3 Memory suggests it fled
  from it once before. This could deepen the Murmur's story (its loneliness, [12](12-life-on-greyborn.md#how-the-murmur-thinks)).
- **A truce?** A future limited mode where Wildborn and Blightborn fight *side by side* against the Clamor.
- **An eighth lineage** isn't needed for pairing anymore (Stillheart is a
  universal partner), but the Clamor could inspire one, for example a lineage the planet grows specifically to resist burning Scald.
