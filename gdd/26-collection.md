# 26 — Collection & Long-Term Progression

What players earn **between** matches, all rooted in the world. **Rule:**
nothing here gives in-match power. Gameplay stays a fair mirror ([10](10-world.md)).
Business model and pricing are out of scope; this covers *what* the
rewards are, not how they're sold. All content is a proposal.

## Kinds of reward

| Reward | What it is in the world | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Growths** | How your creature's body grows: horns, antlers, bark patterns, crystal formations | Model variations per lineage, per side, per Stage |
| **Markings** | Patterns in fur, bark, hide or glass | Colour and pattern layers (within readability rules: never team outline colours) |
| **Calls** | New voices for your call wheel | Call sounds, within each side's palette ([25](25-calls-and-onboarding.md)) |
| **Instincts** | Emotes | New animal behaviours |
| **Memories** | Lore visions found at map sites ([13](13-legends.md)) | Codex entries and Memory-themed rewards |
| **Brood marks** | A team identity symbol scratched into the ground at your base | Shown at match start for premade teams |

### Readability limits on cosmetics
- A cosmetic **never changes a silhouette** beyond agreed limits, never hides
  a weak point, and never uses team outline colours ([14](14-lineage-forms.md#readability-rules-competitive-game)).
- Every cosmetic has a **Wildborn and a Blightborn form**, since players play both sides.

## Lineage Mastery

Long-term progress for each lineage. In the lore, it's how deeply that
answer has taken root in you.

| Mastery rank | Name | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sprout** | Basic Growth variant |
| 2 | **Sapling** | Lineage call voice |
| 3 | **Rooted** | Marking set |
| 4 | **Deep-rooted** | Instinct emote |
| 5 | **Old Growth** | Advanced Growth (Stage 3 variant) |
| 6 | **First Answer's Echo** | A cosmetic that echoes the lineage's First Answer (e.g. Titans with a ridge like the Vertebrae) |

Mastery is earned by playing the lineage on **either side**, so it grows on both.

## Pair Bonds

Long-term progress for **pairs**, to reward pillar 3 (interdependency).

- Playing a pair together with the **same friend** builds a **Pair Bond** between the two of you.
- Bond ranks unlock **shared cosmetics**: matching Growths that only appear
  when you're paired, a shared brood mark, and a joint Synergy visual effect.
- Bonds also exist for **Stillheart Attunement** (if adopted, [21](21-season-2.md#pairing-universal-partner-decided)) with any lineage.

## The Memory Codex

- A book of every Memory found ([13](13-legends.md#how-players-learn-the-history-memories)).
- Pages are grouped by story: *The Fall*, *The First Answers*, *The Hushed*,
  *The Sleeper*, *The Second Light*.
- Finishing a page unlocks a **Memory cosmetic**, e.g. a Breath-themed Verdant
  Growth for finishing *The First Answers*.
- Each season adds new Memories; front results ([18](18-the-answering-war.md)) can unlock unique ones.

## Season rewards

- Each season has a **reward track** themed by its story: Season 1 (glass and
  mountains), Season 2 (ice and sleep), Season 3 (rust and storm).
- **Front results unlock themed sets for everyone**, whichever side won
  ([20](20-season-1.md#front-results)). Everyone shares in the world's history.
- **"I was there" marks:** taking part in a season finale unlocks a permanent
  Memory and a brood mark from that event.

## Profile: your Den

The player's profile is a **den**, a place in the Underroot where your
creature rests between matches.
- Shows your favourite lineage at your highest Mastery Growth.
- Displays found Memories as glowing marks on the walls.
- Shows Pair Bonds as paired claw marks.
- Changes over seasons: frost in Season 2, rust-red scorch marks in Season 3.

## Growths in depth

Growths are the heart of Greyborn's cosmetics, because the game is about
bodies that grow. Every lineage has growth "slots" where its body can vary
without changing its silhouette or hiding its weak point.

| Lineage | Growth slots | Example Growths (Wildborn / Blightborn) |
|---|---|---|
| **Titan** | Shoulder ridges, back crystal, moss coat | *Lichen Mantle* (pale lichen over stone) / *Obsidian Scree* (scattered black glass) |
| **Brawler** | Knuckle plates, mane, shoulder bark | *Stormbark* (bark grooved like lightning) / *Geode Knuckles* (crystal-studded fists) |
| **Verdant** | Antlers, flowers, bark pattern | *Autumn Crown* (antlers in red and gold leaves) / *Frost-glass Crown* (clear glass antlers that catch light) |
| **Hollow** | Chest cavity contents, trailing shadows | *Seed Drift* (floating seeds in the chest) / *Turning Shards* (slow-spinning shards) |
| **Thornrunner** | Thorn length, carapace pattern | *Bramble Coat* / *Needle Glass* |
| **Bonespire** | Bone arrangement on the back | *Reliquary of Small Things* (tiny bones from critters) / *Glass Archive* |
| **Stillheart** | Frost pattern, antler shape | *Snowfall Fur* / *Rime-glass Antlers* |

Growths come in **three Stage versions**, so a Growth chosen in the menu is
visible from Stage 1 and grows with the creature. A player watches their own
cosmetic choice mature over a match, which reinforces the core fantasy.

## Where rewards come from

| Source | Rewards |
|---|---|
| **Playing matches** | Lineage Mastery progress, Pair Bond progress |
| **Finding Memories** | Codex pages and their cosmetics ([38](38-memory-catalogue.md)) |
| **Season reward track** | Themed Growths, Markings, Calls, Instincts |
| **Front results** | Themed sets for everyone, whoever won |
| **Season finales** | "I was there" Memory and brood mark |
| **World events** | Event-themed cosmetics ([39](39-world-events.md)) |
| **Achievements** | Rare Markings for feats (e.g. breaking three weak points in one fight) |

## Readability in practice

The readability limits ([14](14-lineage-forms.md#readability-rules-competitive-game))
are enforced through a **cosmetic review checklist**:

1. **Silhouette test:** the creature in solid black must look identical to the default at match distance.
2. **Weak point test:** the weak point must keep its glow, size and position.
3. **Colour test:** no cosmetic uses either team outline colour, or the
   amber/cyan glow reserved for gameplay meaning ([23](23-art-direction.md)).
4. **Sound test:** cosmetic calls must stay inside the side's sound palette
   and must not mask gameplay sounds.
5. **Mirror test:** the Wildborn and Blightborn versions must be equally readable.

A cosmetic that fails any test doesn't ship.

## The Den in depth

The Den is a hollow in the Underroot where the player's creature rests
between matches. It's the game's main menu, so it should feel like a home.

- **Arrival:** after a match, the creature walks into the Den, still showing
  any scars from the fight, then curls up to rest. Over a few seconds the scars fade.
- **The walls** carry glowing marks for every Memory found. Completed Codex pages form patterns.
- **The floor** has paired claw marks for every Pair Bond rank, so friends see
  their history together when they visit.
- **The roof** opens to the sky through a crack. It shows the current season's sky: the Fall-line, the red streak, the Lantern moon.
- **Seasonal change:** frost in Rime, ash drifting in during Ash, flowers in
  Bloom, heat shimmer in Fever. The Den follows the planet's year ([29](29-the-planets-year.md)).
- **Companions:** some world events add small creatures that live in the Den,
  such as a Mossback calf from Calving Days or a Lantern Lizard that lights the corner.

## Why there's no power for sale

Greyborn's mirror rule says the two sides are always equal. That would mean
nothing if a player could buy an advantage. Every reward is about **who your
creature is**, not **how strong it is**. This keeps ranked play fair and
keeps the focus where the game's pillars put it: on growth inside the match.
