# 31 — Evolution Branches

The source pages ([00](00-source-pages.md)) show an **Evolution Branching &
Progression Track** splitting into **Aggression** and **Tactical Control**,
ending in **six leaf abilities**. [03](03-evolution-system.md#evolution-branching-progressive-tree)
set up the structure; this chapter fills it in for every lineage. All
abilities here are proposals.

## Structure (recap)

| Level | Choice | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **L10** (Stage 2) | **Aggression** or **Tactical Control** | Branch stats (below) and one **branch upgrade** to an existing ability |
| **L15** | (automatic) | A second branch upgrade |
| **L20** (Stage 3) | **One of three Ultimate Forms** in your branch | Your Stage 3 ultimate ability, and a visible change to your Ascendant |

**Branch stats (all lineages):**
- **Aggression:** +10% Power, +5% Speed. Kills drop **+15% cores**.
- **Tactical Control:** +15% Control, +10% Health. Node captures are **+25% faster**.

**Mirror rule:** Wildborn and Blightborn have the same branches and
ultimates. Only visuals and sound differ ([14](14-lineage-forms.md)).

**Design goal for each set of three ultimates:** one for **fights**, one for
**objectives**, and one that's **unusual**, so every branch offers a real choice.

## Balance notes

- Every lineage has at least one **objective ultimate** in each branch, so no
  branch abandons the territory game.
- **Ultimates that reshape space** (Fault Line, Living Wall, Brawl Circle,
  Ossuary Maze, Winter's Wall) are the riskiest. They need cooldowns long
  enough that they can't lock down a point all match.
- **Step Between** (Hollow, teleport the whole team) and **Shatterfist**
  (Titan, one-hit weak-point break) are the likeliest to be too strong.
  Prototype them first.
- The source pages' Synergy unlocks (L12, L15, L18) fall *between* the L10 and
  L20 branch milestones. Players gain something meaningful at L10, L12, L15,
  L18 and L20, so the late game keeps a steady rhythm of power.
