# 12 — Life on Greyborn

How life works on the planet: the Base Forms' life cycle, the plants players
use, weather, and the biology of the Murmur. All content here is a proposal.

## The Kith life cycle

Base Forms are **native creatures of Greyborn** that occur naturally, like the
rest of its wildlife (decided by the director). Proposed biology:

1. **Budding.** Base Forms grow from **birth-nodes**: swollen root knots that
   ripen like fruit. When one splits open, a Base Form steps out fully formed.
2. **Broods.** Birth-nodes ripen in clusters, so Base Forms emerge in groups
   of 3–6 that stay together by instinct. A 4v4 team is a brood.
3. **Strays.** Most Base Forms live their whole lives in the wild as Strays
   (see [09](09-wildlife.md)). They feed on SAP-rich plants and avoid predators.
4. **The Answering.** When the planet needs defenders, it **calls** certain
   broods. Those Base Forms feel the call as hunger and drive, and begin to
   evolve. These are the Wildborn.
5. **Taken.** When a birth-node ripens in blighted soil, the Murmur reaches the
   Base Forms first. They emerge already listening to the whispers. These are
   the Blightborn.
6. **Ascension.** An evolved creature that reaches Stage 3 becomes an
   Ascendant, a living answer (or a corrupted copy of one).
7. **Return.** When an Ascendant dies, its body sinks back into the soil and
   becomes part of the planet. In the Bone Flats, its bones join the planet's memory.

**Gameplay tie-in:** player bases are proposed as great **birth-node
clusters**, the place the brood grew from. Respawning is budding again. And
"returning to base" means returning to the place you were born to grow.

### The Kith

The species is named the **Kith** (decided 2026-10-02): an old word for
kin. A team is "a brood of Kith"; wild ones are Strays; those taken by the
Murmur at birth are the "Hushed Kith". In gameplay, a Kith at Levels 1–2 is
in its **Base Form**.

## Flora: plants players interact with

*The full catalogue of 26 plants is in [44 — Flora](44-flora.md). A full portrait of the Kith is in [45 — The Kith](45-the-kith.md).*

Proposed interactable plants, placed by level designers. Each one is a small
tactical tool.

| Plant | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Sapbloom** | Rootwilds, Heartwood | Eat to heal 15% Health over 5 s. Regrows in 60 s |
| **Thunderpod** | Ashen Steppe | Seed pod that bursts when struck: knockback in a 4 m radius |
| **Veilfern** | Hollow Mire | Tall fronds. Standing inside hides you from the minimap and mutes your sound signature |
| **Bonevine** | Bone Flats, Spirecliffs | Climbable vines on fossils and cliffs: vertical shortcuts |
| **Lanternmoss** | Underroot | Glows when anything walks past it. A natural tripwire |
| **Rimecap** | Rimewastes | Frost mushroom. Bursting it freezes the ground into a slippery patch |
| **Embergrass** | Cinderveil | Catches fire when a Brawler or Titan charges through it, leaving a burning trail |
| **Glasscap** | Blight (any) | A Murmur fungus. Blightborn eat it to heal. Wildborn take damage from it |
| **Heartbloom** | Heartwood groves | Rare flower. Wildborn eat it to heal. Blightborn take damage from it |

Glasscap and Heartbloom are mirrors, like everything else on the two sides.

## Weather and time of day

Each match rolls one **weather** condition and a **time of day**. Proposal: these
change visibility and sound, not raw damage, so they stay fair.

| Weather | Regions | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | Any | None |
| **Ashfall** | Ashen Steppe, Cinderveil | Visibility −40%. Sound signatures carry 25% farther (you hear more than you see) |
| **Rime Fog** | Rimewastes, Hollow Mire | Visibility −50% at range. Nodes capture 10% slower (cold soil) |
| **Sap Rain** | Rootwilds, Heartwood | SAP income +20% for both teams. Roots spread faster in the rain |
| **Shard Storm** | Shattered Coast, Starwound zones | Meteor glass falls. Blight spreads faster. Random damage zones |
| **Breathing Night** | Any | Darkness. Lantern Lizards, Lanternmoss and glowing Blight become very important |

## The biology of the Murmur

The Murmur is one mind spread across countless bodies and growths. It
**mirrors the planet** in structure but not in nature:

| The planet | The Murmur |
|---|---|
| Roots (nerves) | **Blight** (crystal lattice) |
| SAP (blood) | **Glow**: cyan-violet light that pulses through Blight |
| Nodes (clusters of awareness) | **Whisper-nodes**: crystal clusters where the Murmur's thought gathers |
| Heartwood (heart) | **The Seed** at the centre of the Starwound |
| Birth-nodes | Blighted birth-nodes ("taken" clutches) |
| Speaks through instinct | Speaks through **whispers**, a sound every creature can hear and only the Blightborn understand |

### Stages of Blight (on the ground)

1. **Glaze:** a thin glassy film. It looks frosted. Easy to purge.
2. **Lattice:** crystal growth ankle- to knee-high. It whispers and spreads.
3. **Spire:** tall crystal columns that light up the night. A fully claimed node.
4. **Glasswaste:** land that has been Spire for too long turns permanently to
   glass. This is how regions are lost (lore only; it doesn't happen in matches).

In a match, these stages are just the **visual states of Blightborn territory**
as it grows over time. They have no extra rules, so the two sides stay mirrored.

### How the Murmur thinks

- It **learns**. Every creature it takes teaches it a new shape. That's why it
  can copy all six lineages.
- It is **curious** about the Ascendants above all. They are the planet's best
  work, and it wants them.
- It **never hurries**. The Murmur has been spreading for an age. A lost
  battle doesn't trouble it; it simply tries again elsewhere.
- It may be **lonely**. A mind that crossed the stars in a stone, with no voice
  but its own, endlessly copying the shapes of others… (A thread for future
  story, kept deliberately unresolved.)

## How life feels on Greyborn

Everything on Greyborn is connected to the planet. Plants drink its SAP;
grazers eat the plants; predators eat the grazers; and everything that dies
sinks back into the soil and becomes the planet again. Nothing is wasted.
The Kith understand this without words. They feel the planet underfoot as a
pressure, a warmth, sometimes a call.

The Murmur breaks this circle. Blighted creatures don't eat, don't die in the
same way, and don't return to the soil; they shatter into glass dust that
never becomes anything else. Where the Murmur is strong, the circle of life
stops turning, and the world goes quiet.

## A Kith's first day, in matches and in the wild

| | In the wild | In a match |
|---|---|---|
| **Budding** | A birth-node ripens over days, then splits | The match starts; the Cradle or Clutch splits |
| **First food** | Sapbloom petals | Critters, Strays, Sapbloom |
| **First danger** | An Ashfang pack | An enemy brood, or the same Ashfang pack |
| **The call** | Rarely comes | Comes immediately: the planet (or the Murmur) wants this brood now |
| **Growth** | Slow, over seasons, or never | Twenty levels in twenty minutes |

The match is a compressed, intense version of a Kith's life: everything a
wild Kith might do over a lifetime, a called Kith does in one battle.

## Weather in depth

**Ashfall** turns the air grey and soft. Sound travels farther than sight,
so players hunt by ear. **Rime Fog** hangs low over cold ground; shapes
loom out of it suddenly. **Sap Rain** falls warm and amber, and every surface
glistens. **Shard Storm** is the strangest: glittering glass falling from a
clear sky, chiming as it lands. **Breathing Night** is darkness lit only by the
moon, the Fall-line and the living glow of the world: Lanternmoss, nerve-light, and Blight.

## The Murmur's biology, as a scientist might see it

If anyone on Greyborn could study the Murmur, they would note:

- **It isn't a fungus, a crystal or an animal.** It's a mind that grows as crystal.
- **It needs SAP** to grow, but doesn't eat it like an animal; it absorbs it through Blight.
- **It has no single brain.** Thought gathers in whisper-nodes, and densest in the Lattice Sea.
- **It copies by touch.** A creature taken by Blight is studied, and the Murmur learns its shape.
- **It can now create** (Season 5): something it had never done on Greyborn before.
