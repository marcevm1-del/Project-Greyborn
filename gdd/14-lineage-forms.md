# 14 — Lineage Forms: Wildborn & Blightborn

An art and audio brief for how each of the six lineages looks and sounds at
every Stage, on both sides. All content here is a proposal.

## Readability rules (competitive game)

Because the two sides are a **mirror** (decided), these rules matter:

1. **Identical silhouettes and hitboxes.** A Wildborn Titan and a Blightborn
   Titan at the same Stage have the same outline, size and animation timing. A
   player must be able to read *what* an enemy is doing from its shape alone.
2. **Side is shown by material, colour and sound, never by shape.**
   - **Wildborn:** organic materials (bark, moss, horn, bone, amber sap), warm
     earth tones, amber glow.
   - **Blightborn:** crystalline materials (black glass, shard, chitin shine),
     cold tones, cyan-violet glow.
3. **Weak points glow** in the side's colour (amber vs. cyan-violet) so they're easy to spot.
4. **Your own team is always shown with a consistent outline colour**, whichever
   side you're on. Side colours are flavour; team colours are information.
5. **Colour-blind support:** side material contrast (matte organic vs. glossy
   crystal) has to read even in greyscale.

## Stage progression (both sides)

| Stage | Wildborn | Blightborn |
|---|---|---|
| **Base Form (L1–2)** | Small grey humanoid, budded from root. Moss-flecked skin, amber eyes | The same humanoid with glassy patches along the spine and faint cyan veins. Eyes flicker violet |
| **Stage 1 (L3–9)** | The lineage's first shape grows *out of* the Base Form, rough and young | The lineage shape is grown *over* the Base Form as a crystal shell. Cracks show the glow beneath |
| **Stage 2 (L10–19)** | Full lineage features. Growths become detailed (the source art's "growth-detailed forms") | Crystal plates replace growth. Whispers start to be audible nearby |
| **Stage 3 (L20)** | **Ascendant:** "hyper-rooted". Roots, horns and bark spread from the body into the ground it walks on | **Hushed Ascendant:** "hyper-crystalline". Glass spires grow from the body, and it leaves a trail of Glaze |

## The six lineages

### Titan
- **Wildborn:** a stone-and-moss colossus. Its back holds a ridge of amber
  crystal (the weak point) like sap set in rock. Stage 3: small trees grow on
  its shoulders, and each step leaves a root-print.
- **Blightborn:** a black glass colossus with a lattice of cyan light inside.
  Its spine-crystal glows violet. Stage 3: it resembles the **Glass Range**,
  and the ground rings like a bell under its steps.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = deep grinding rock. Blightborn = deep tone like a
  struck glass bowl.

### Brawler
- **Wildborn:** a knuckle-walking beast in bark armour, with thick fur at its
  joints. Its chest plate (weak point) is a knot of heartwood. Stage 3: its
  fists are covered in bark that cracks and regrows with each punch.
- **Blightborn:** crystal-studded fists and shard spikes along its back. Its
  chest plate is a glowing geode. Stage 3: every punch scatters glass dust.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = snarl and breaking branches. Blightborn = snarl doubled
  by a whisper a fraction of a second behind.

### Verdant
- **Wildborn:** a stag-like creature woven of living wood, with flowering
  antlers. Its bloom pod (weak point) is a large amber flower. Stage 3: a
  walking grove, with birds and insects living in its branches.
- **Blightborn:** the same stag shape with glass branches in place of antlers
  and crystal flowers that never open. Its bloom pod is a crystal bud. Stage 3:
  the **Lattice Grove**'s child, a slow-moving garden of glass.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = creaking wood, rustling leaves. Blightborn = wind
  chimes, tinkling glass.

### Hollow
- **Wildborn:** a thin shadowy form with a hollow chest cavity (the weak
  point) full of soft darkness and floating seeds. Stage 3: a floating shape of
  roots and darkness, like a cave turned inside out.
- **Blightborn:** the same thin form, but the chest cavity is full of slowly
  turning shards. Stage 3: the **Unspoken**'s heir, half-invisible, outlined
  in faint violet light.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = silence that sounds pressing, a muffled heartbeat.
  Blightborn = a chorus of faint whispers just below hearing.

### Thornrunner
- **Wildborn:** a long-legged quadruped covered in thorns, with its abdomen
  sac (weak point) glowing amber. Stage 3: thorn-tails and hooked legs, almost
  insectile, but still part animal.
- **Blightborn:** the same shape, but the thorns are glass needles and its
  abdomen sac is a pulsing cyan light. Stage 3: it trails glittering dust, like
  the **Endless Pursuit**.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = skittering, thorn clicks. Blightborn = the skittering
  of glass on stone, like ice cracking.

### Bonespire
- **Wildborn:** a hunched beast with fossil-bone spines. Its marrow spire (weak
  point) is a pale bone tower on its back. Stage 3: a walking reliquary of bones
  from every creature it has hunted.
- **Blightborn:** spines of black glass, with shards where marrow should be.
  The marrow spire glows cyan. Stage 3: like the **Archive**, it carries glass
  copies of its victims' bones.
- **Sound:** Wildborn = bone clicking, a hollow crack when it fires. Blightborn
  = glass ringing, a shatter when it fires.

## Stage transformation moments

The source pages say Stage changes are amplified moments. Proposed treatment:

- **Wildborn:** roots burst from the ground, wrap the creature for 1.5 s, and
  fall away to reveal the new form. A deep tremor is heard map-wide.
- **Blightborn:** a crystal cocoon forms in an instant and shatters outward,
  revealing the new form. A ringing chime and a rush of whispers are heard map-wide.
- Both last exactly **1.5 s** (invulnerable but rooted in place, per [03](03-evolution-system.md)).

## Material reference sheets

Every lineage is built from a small set of materials. These sheets keep the
two sides consistent.

| Material | Wildborn | Blightborn |
|---|---|---|
| **Skin** | Grey, moss-flecked, matte | Grey with glassy patches, slight sheen |
| **Armour** | Bark, stone, horn | Black glass, chitin, crystal plate |
| **Growths** | Leaves, moss, flowers, antlers of wood | Crystal spires, glass needles, shard clusters |
| **Inner light** | Amber, warm, like sap lit from within | Cyan-violet, cold, pulsing |
| **Eyes** | Amber, soft glow | Violet, flickering |
| **Weak point** | Amber crystal, sap-filled, glowing | Cyan geode, pulsing |

## Transformation effects, lineage by lineage

The Stage-change moment ([03](03-evolution-system.md#the-feel-of-a-transformation))
has a lineage-specific flourish on each side.

| Lineage | Wildborn flourish | Blightborn flourish |
|---|---|---|
| Titan | Boulders rise and fuse onto the body | Black glass erupts and sets like cooling lava |
| Brawler | Bark splits and regrows thicker across the fists | Geodes crack open along the arms |
| Verdant | A burst of leaves and petals | A burst of glass petals that ring as they fall |
| Hollow | Darkness folds inward, then opens | Shards spiral into the chest cavity |
| Thornrunner | Thorns lengthen with a creak | Needles shoot out with a chiming snap |
| Bonespire | Bones rattle up from the ground and lock onto the back | Glass bones grow out of the spine |
| Stillheart | Frost spreads and thickens into ice | Frost turns to black glass |

## Team colour examples

Because team colours are separate from side colours
([23](23-art-direction.md#colour-palette)), every creature carries both:

- **Side** is shown by material and inner light (amber or cyan).
- **Team** is shown by a crisp outline: your team always one colour (e.g. a
  pale gold), the enemy always another (e.g. a sharp red), whatever side you're on.

So a Blightborn player sees their own Blightborn teammates with a gold
outline and the Wildborn enemy with a red outline, and next match, playing
Wildborn, they see their own Wildborn teammates in gold and the Blightborn in
red. **Outline = friend or foe; material = which side of the story.**

## Common art mistakes to avoid

1. **Giving the two sides different silhouettes.** Even a slightly different
   horn shape makes the mirror harder to read.
2. **Using team colours as side colours.** Players will confuse "enemy" with "Blightborn".
3. **Over-glowing.** If everything glows, weak points stop standing out.
4. **Making Blightborn look evil.** They're strange and beautiful, not monstrous.
5. **Forgetting Stage 1.** It's the form players see most often in early fights; it needs as much care as Stage 3.

## Every lineage, Stage by Stage

**Titan.**
*Stage 1 (Wildborn):* a hunched Kith with stone plates fused across its
shoulders and back, still recognisably small, its amber eyes peering out from
under a brow of rock. *(Blightborn):* the same, but the plates are black
glass with cyan cracks, and its eyes flicker violet.
*Stage 2:* a heavy, knuckle-walking colossus. Wildborn: moss carpets its back,
and the amber crystal ridge of its weak point runs down its spine.
Blightborn: smooth glass planes with veins of light running upward.
*Stage 3:* a walking mountain. Wildborn: trees on its shoulders, roots trailing
from its feet. Blightborn: a glass mountain like the Glass Range, ringing with each step.

**Brawler.**
*Stage 1:* a wiry, long-armed Kith with hardened knuckles and a bark-plated
chest. Blightborn: geode knuckles, glass plates.
*Stage 2:* a gorilla-like beast. Wildborn: thick fur at the joints, bark
armour, a heartwood chest plate. Blightborn: crystal studs on the fists, shard spikes along the back, a geode chest.
*Stage 3:* an armoured berserker. Wildborn: bark cracking and regrowing with
every punch. Blightborn: glass dust bursting from every blow.

**Verdant.**
*Stage 1:* a slender Kith with small antler buds and moss spreading down its legs.
Blightborn: glass buds, frost-like Glaze on its legs.
*Stage 2:* a stag of living wood with flowering antlers and an amber bloom pod.
Blightborn: glass antlers, crystal flowers that never open, a crystal bud.
*Stage 3:* a walking grove with birds in its branches. Blightborn: a slow garden of glass.

**Hollow.**
*Stage 1:* a thin Kith with a small dark opening in its chest. Blightborn: the opening holds tiny turning shards.
*Stage 2:* a shadowy, floating wraith with a chest cavity full of soft darkness and seeds.
Blightborn: full of slowly turning shards.
*Stage 3:* a cave turned inside out, floating. Blightborn: half-invisible, outlined in violet light.

**Thornrunner.**
*Stage 1:* a low, spiny Kith with extra legs beginning to form along its sides.
Blightborn: glass needles instead of thorns.
*Stage 2:* a long-legged, insectile quadruped with an amber abdomen sac.
Blightborn: glass needles, a pulsing cyan sac.
*Stage 3:* a bladed horror with hooked legs. Blightborn: trailing glittering dust.

**Bonespire.**
*Stage 1:* a hunched Kith with small bone spikes rising from its back.
Blightborn: glass spikes.
*Stage 2:* a ballista-beast with fossil spines and a pale marrow spire.
Blightborn: black glass spines, a glowing cyan spire.
*Stage 3:* a walking reliquary of bones. Blightborn: carrying glass copies of its victims' bones, like the Archive.

**Stillheart.**
*Stage 1:* a curled, frost-furred Kith, sleepy-eyed. Blightborn: frost replaced by fine glass needles.
*Stage 2:* a long-limbed, ice-antlered sloth-like creature with a visible heart.
Blightborn: black glass antlers, a cyan heart.
*Stage 3:* a walking glacier with a slow-beating heart in its icy chest.
Blightborn: a glacier of dark crystal, its heart pulsing cyan-violet.

## Player Blightborn vs. the Hushed Answers

Players' Blightborn Ascendants and the Murmur's Hushed Answers are both glass
copies of the planet's lineages, so they must be clearly distinguished:

| | Player Blightborn | Hushed Answers |
|---|---|---|
| Size | Stage 3 at most | Far larger than any Stage 3 |
| Kith traces | Keep the Kith's eyes at Stage 1–2 | No trace of a Kith at all |
| Team outline | Yes | None (neutral encounter) |
| Movement | Animal, alive | Slow, perfect, mechanical |
| Sound | Whispers in harmony | A single deep tone |

## Forms in the Truce

In the Truce mode, a creature standing on **braided ground** for long enough
shows a cosmetic hint of the other side: a Wildborn Titan's moss catching a
few flecks of glass; a Blightborn Verdant's glass antlers sprouting a single
real leaf. It fades when they leave the braid. A small visual reward for standing together.

## Why Stage 1 matters most

Stage 1 is on screen for the first third of every match, in every player's
early fights. It's the stage where players first see what lineage the enemy
picked. It deserves as much art time as Stage 3, and it carries the
important job of keeping the Kith visible inside the new form.

## Stage changes, shown from the inside

The player sees their own transformation differently from everyone else:

- **The camera** pulls back smoothly to fit the new size.
- **The HUD** briefly shows the new Stage's silhouette.
- **The sound** comes from all around: for Wildborn, roots tearing through
  the earth on every side; for Blightborn, crystal growing in a ring.
- **The first step** in the new form is heavier and louder, and the player
  feels the difference immediately.

## Forms across the Turns

| Turn | Wildborn forms | Blightborn forms |
|---|---|---|
| Bloom | Flowers open on Verdants and in Titans' moss | Crystal flowers bloom faster, sharper |
| Ash | A coat of ash settles on every creature | Ash dulls the glass slightly |
| Rime | Frost on fur and bark; breath visible | Frost on glass; cold blue edges |
| Fever | Bark parched and cracked; panting | Glass glowing warmer, slightly cracked |

These are cosmetic overlays only, and never hide weak points or change silhouettes.

## Checklist for a new lineage

When a new lineage is designed (as Stillheart was), its forms need:

1. A **Kith-based Stage 1** that keeps the Kith's eyes and ears.
2. A **distinct silhouette** at every Stage, unlike any existing lineage.
3. A **weak point** in a place that matches its weakness.
4. **Wildborn and Blightborn materials** for every Stage.
5. A **transformation flourish** for each side.
6. A **sound palette** for each side.
7. A **First Answer** (or equivalent) for its lore and its Mastery Echo cosmetic.

## Lineage forms in one line each

- **Titan:** a Kith becoming a mountain.
- **Brawler:** a Kith becoming a storm with fists.
- **Verdant:** a Kith becoming a walking forest.
- **Hollow:** a Kith becoming a cave of silence.
- **Thornrunner:** a Kith becoming the hunt itself.
- **Bonespire:** a Kith becoming a reliquary of memory.
- **Stillheart:** a Kith becoming winter, with a heart you can see.

On the Blightborn side, each of these is the same shape, made of glass, and
humming with the Murmur's whisper.

## Closing principle

One body, two materials, three Stages, seven lineages: the forms system is
simple at its core, so that players can always read what they're fighting,
and rich in its details, so that every creature feels alive.
