# 36 — Greyborn at a Glance

A five-minute summary of the world and the game for anyone new to the
project. **Decided** facts come from the director or the source pages;
everything else points to the chapter where it's proposed.

## The game (source pages)

- **4v4 team PvP** about **evolution and territory**.
- Every player starts small and evolves through **20 levels and 3 Stages** into an **Ascendant**.
- **Evolve by:** getting kills (evolution cores), destroying Enemy Cores, and returning to base (risky but strategic).
- **Six lineages in three pairs:** Titan + Brawler, Verdant + Hollow, Thornrunner + Bonespire. Pairs unlock **Synergies** at Levels 12, 15 and 18.
- **Territory:** nodes (Captured / Inaccessible / Vulnerable), Resource Hubs (Defense 1–10), SAP, Territorial Influence.
- **Branches:** Aggression or Tactical Control, ending in six final forms per lineage.
- Tagline: ***Kills fuel evolution. Returns fuel tactical choice. Dominate the map.***

## The world (decided by the director)

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Greyborn is a planet** | Alive and aware. Primal nature fantasy. Only creatures, no cultures, no words |
| **The players' species: the Kith** | Natural creatures of Greyborn. They bud from root knots in broods; a team is a brood |
| **The Fall** | A meteorite, the **Starwound**, struck the planet carrying a hive-mind disease, **the Murmur** |
| **Two sides** | **Wildborn** (the planet's response) vs. **Blightborn** (the infected). Same lineages, mirror gameplay, assigned per match |
| **The infection** | Blight replaces roots; wildlife can be infected; crater zones on maps; nature fights back |
| **Wildlife** | Four-, two-, six- and eight-legged creatures, plus insects. 20–50+ types with contrasting creatures |
| **The War Map** | Moves by **community goals** each season |
| **Stillheart** | A seventh lineage (from Season 3), a **universal partner** |
| **The second light** | A **rival hive mind** arrives in Season 3 |
| **The Murmur's past** | The Clamor devoured its home world; a fragment fled in the meteorite; the Clamor followed it |
| **The Murmur learns to create** | Its first creation is the Glass Flower (Season 4), shaped from a memory of home. Season 5 is the turning point |
| **Why the Kith** | The First Answers were Kith; every Kith carries all the lineages sleeping inside it |
| **The Quiet World** | The Murmur's lost home: a crystal world where it was the native life, until the Clamor devoured it |
| **Season 6: The Roaring Comes** | The Clamor in full force; the planet and the Murmur face it together |
| **Stillheart's purpose** | The planet's answer to the Clamor, put to sleep because the planet feared what it was for |
| **Season 7: the truce breaks** | The war returns harder; one small patch of braided ground, the Last Braid, survives |

## The war in one paragraph

Long ago the Starwound fell in the far north. The Murmur, which can only
copy and corrupt, spread outward as glass Blight and began taking the
planet's creatures, even copying its first six Ascendants (the First
Answers) as glass guardians (the Hushed Answers). The planet slept to heal,
then woke and began calling broods of Kith to evolve and fight: the
Answering. Every match is one battle in that war. The Murmur wants the
**Heartwood**, the planet's heart in the far south, where it has never been
able to grow. ([10](10-world.md), [13](13-legends.md), [24](24-the-three-minds.md))

## The five rules that hold everything together

1. **The mirror:** Wildborn and Blightborn always have identical rules and power. Only look, sound and story differ.
2. **No words in the world:** story is told through landscape, creature behaviour, sound and Memories.
3. **Colour and glow mean something:** amber is the planet, cyan-violet is the Murmur, rust-red is the Clamor; team colours are separate.
4. **Nothing outside a match gives power inside one.**
5. **Mysteries stay open on purpose** until the director decides: the Sleeper, the Glass Flower, what's under the First Root, what's inside the Seed.
