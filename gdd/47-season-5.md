# 47 — Season 5: The First Made Thing

The **turning point** of the story (decided, 2026-10-02). The Murmur, which
could only copy, has learned to **create** (decided). This season shows what
that means for the war. Everything below the decisions is a proposal, and the
**mirror rule holds**: both playable sides stay equal.

## The other half of the truth

Season 4's finale revealed one half of the Glass Flower's truth
([30](30-season-4.md#the-central-mystery)). Season 5 opens by revealing the other:
- If players learned it was **a shape from far away**, Season 5 reveals that
  **the Murmur made it**: it's the first thing it ever created.
- If players learned it was **the first made thing**, Season 5 reveals that
  **it's shaped like a flower from the Murmur's lost home**.

Either way, by week 3 the full truth is out: **the Murmur made something
new, and what it made was a memory of home.** ([37](37-the-clamor.md#the-clamor-and-the-murmur-what-really-happened))

## How the world changes

1. **The whispers change.** Hushed Kith begin to **hum a melody**: the first
   tune the Murmur has ever had that isn't copied. Players on the Blightborn
   side hear it under the whispers. (The music system gains a third theme,
   [19](19-sound-and-music.md#music).)
2. **Glass Gardens appear.** In a few places, Blight stops consuming and
   starts *growing*: glass gardens that are beautiful, strange, and not shaped
   like anything on Greyborn. Are they still an infection? The planet doesn't seem sure.
3. **The planet answers by creating.** For the first time since the Sleeper,
   the planet grows **new kinds of life**, not to fight the Murmur directly
   but to answer it, as if in conversation.
4. **The Clamor hears it.** Creation makes a sound the Clamor can follow.
   Landfalls ([22](22-season-3.md)) grow **larger** through the season. The
   Murmur's turning point puts it, and the planet, in more danger.

## New mechanic: Seedbeds

Creation becomes something **both sides do** in matches.

- Every map gains **2 Seedbeds**, neutral sites in mirrored positions.
- A team that holds a Seedbed for **60 s** grows a **creation** there:
  - **Wildborn** grow a **Bloom Totem**: a fast-growing living tree.
  - **Blightborn** grow a **Glass Garden**: a cluster of new, unfamiliar crystal plants.
- Both creations give the **same strength** of effect, with different flavour:

| Creation | Effect (identical power) |
|---|---|
| Bloom Totem / Glass Garden | Allies within 15 m regenerate 2% Health/s and the team's passive core income is +10% while it stands |

- The enemy can **destroy** a creation (it has Health like a small Hub), and then grow its own there.
- **Why:** it puts the season's story, two minds learning to create, directly
  into play, and it's a new, symmetrical objective type.

## New creatures

The Murmur's first original creatures, and the planet's answers. Each pair is
balanced: the Murmur's creation favours Blightborn exactly as much as the
planet's answer favours Wildborn.

| # | Creature | Made by | Body plan | Tier | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 | **Prismwings** | The Murmur | Insect | I | Glass moths that never existed before. On Blight territory, their light **reveals Wildborn** within 10 m |
| 56 | **Dawnwings** | The planet | Insect | I | The planet's answer: moths of amber light. On root territory, they **reveal Blightborn** within 10 m |
| 57 | **Lattice Fawn** | The Murmur | Four-legged | II | A delicate glass deer with no copy on Greyborn. It **grows Glass Gardens** wherever it rests (cosmetic outside Seedbeds) and follows Blightborn |
| 58 | **Firstlight Fawn** | The planet | Four-legged | II | The planet's answer: a deer of living wood and flowers. It grows **Bloom shoots** where it rests and follows Wildborn |

These are the first creatures in the game that are **not neutral wildlife and
not converted**: they are **born on a side**. They're balanced as exact mirrors.

## Finale mode: The Garden or the Wound

- **Map:** a special version of **The Ash Sea** ([42](42-new-maps.md#map-9--the-ash-sea-4v4)),
  with the first Glass Garden at its centre and Seedbeds all around it.
- **Objective (same for both sides):** grow and protect the most creations
  across the map's Seedbeds by the end. Creations can be destroyed and regrown.
- **Midway, a Clamor Landfall** hits the map. Both sides' creations are
  threatened by the Scald, so both have to decide whether to defend, attack
  each other, or fight the Clamor.
- **Community result:**
  - **The Garden stands** (Blightborn grew more): the first Glass Garden
    becomes a permanent region on the War Map. It's a sign the Murmur might be
    able to live on Greyborn without consuming it.
  - **The Wound is closed** (Wildborn grew more): the planet grows over the
    Glass Garden, absorbing it. The planet has learned something from the
    Murmur's creation. It's a sign the two minds might one day understand each other.
  - Both outcomes keep the story moving toward the same big question.

## The question the turning point opens

**If the Murmur can create, does it still need to take the planet?**

Season 5 doesn't answer this. It makes it the central question for everything
after: a possible future truce, a possible new war, or something neither the
planet nor the Murmur has imagined yet. And the Clamor, drawn by the sound of
creation, is coming in greater force.
