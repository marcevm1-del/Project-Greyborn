# 34 — Creatures of the Far Regions

The first 42 creatures ([09](09-wildlife.md)) live mostly in the six core
biomes. Season 4 added four more ([30](30-season-4.md#new-creatures-for-bloom)).
This chapter adds **natives** for the regions that so far only had variants:
the **Rimewastes, Cinderveil, Shattered Coast** and **Heartwood**. Same rules
as before (tiers, temperament, affinity, Blighted forms). All are proposals.

| # | Creature | Region | Body plan | Tier | Temperament | Affinity | What makes it matter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 47 | **Drift Owlbear** | Rimewastes | Two-legged | III | Territorial | Brawler | A huge white-furred creature that sleeps under snow. Stepping on its drift wakes it, angry |
| 48 | **Hoarfrost Mites** | Rimewastes | Eight-legged | I | Swarm | Stillheart | Tiny frost spiders. Where they gather, the ground freezes into slippery patches |
| 49 | **Cinder Salamander** | Cinderveil | Four-legged | II | Skittish | Titan | Lives in lava. When killed, it leaves a **cooling-rock** stepping stone across a lava channel |
| 50 | **Ash Vulture** | Cinderveil | Two-legged (winged) | I | Scavenger | Bonespire | Circles above fights. **Wherever vultures circle, something is about to die**, a readable sign of a fight in progress |
| 51 | **Tidal Strider** | Shattered Coast | Six-legged | II | Passive | Hollow | Walks on the surface of the water. Players can ride beneath it across flooded causeways at high tide |
| 52 | **Shard Gull** | Shattered Coast | Two-legged (winged) | I | Skittish flock | Thornrunner | Collects glittering meteor shards. Killing one drops a small Starshard (bonus cores) |
| 53 | **Heartwood Elk** | Heartwood | Four-legged | IV | Passive (apex) | Verdant | The planet's own messenger, enormous, with antlers like branching trees. **It can't be harmed.** When it appears in a Heartwood grove, roots spread 3× faster there for 30 s, for whoever holds the grove |
| 54 | **Amber Beetles** | Heartwood | Insect | I | Passive | Verdant | Carry droplets of SAP on their backs. Killing them gives SAP instead of cores |

## Notes

- **Two winged creatures** (Ash Vulture, Shard Gull) count as two-legged, in
  keeping with the director's body plans. Greyborn has birds, but no insects
  are ridden and nothing flies higher than tree height. The skies belong to the Fall-line.
- **The Heartwood Elk** is the first **apex that can't be harmed.** It isn't a
  threat; it's a blessing that both teams want to be standing near. In lore,
  it's the closest thing the planet has to a voice.
- **Stillheart affinity** (Hoarfrost Mites) is the first creature tied to the seventh lineage.

## Blighted forms

| # | Creature | Blighted form | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 47 | Drift Owlbear | **Glass-furred Owlbear** | Its drift is Glaze. It wakes only for Wildborn |
| 48 | Hoarfrost Mites | **Shard Mites** | Their patches whisper; Blightborn don't slip on them |
| 49 | Cinder Salamander | **Obsidian Salamander** | Leaves a glass stepping stone that only Blightborn can use |
| 50 | Ash Vulture | **Glass Vulture** | Circles only above Wildborn who are about to die |
| 51 | Tidal Strider | **Shard Strider** | Hides Blightborn beneath it |
| 52 | Shard Gull | **Hush Gull** | Silent. Its shards are larger |
| 53 | Heartwood Elk | **None.** The Murmur has never touched one. Infecting it is impossible | |
| 54 | Amber Beetles | **Glow Beetles** | Carry Glow (the Murmur's light) instead of SAP |

The **Heartwood Elk** is the only creature without a Blighted form. That's a
deliberate exception that says something about the Heartwood: the Murmur can't reach it.
