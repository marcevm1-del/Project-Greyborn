# Bone Flats

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Landforms | Dead-flat salt plain; fossil ribs, skulls and spines 5–60 m tall; the Elder Ribs (a ribcage the size of a city) |
| Ground | White salt crust, Saltcrust Lichen (loud underfoot), bone fields that slow movement (Spec hazard) |
| Light | Overexposed at noon (Spec: salt glare), cool white. Morning and dusk are the readable hours |

## From the atlas

A white salt plain covered in the fossil bones of creatures far larger than
any alive today. The planet remembers everything that ever lived on it.
- **Hazards:** salt glare (reduced vision at noon), bone fields that slow movement.
- **Wildlife:** Marrowhounds, [[Bonewright|Bonewrights]], Trundlebacks, Bone Harvestmen, Siege Beetles, Marrow Mantises.
- **Murmur:** the Murmur hunts here for old bones to copy. Blight spreads along the fossils.
- **Landmark:** **[[The Elder Ribs]]**, a fossil ribcage the size of a city. Some say it belonged to the first [[Titan]].
- **Map feel:** open with clusters of bone cover. [[Bonespire]] country.
