# 02 — Ascendant Roster

The source pages name **six lineages**, grouped into **three synergy pairs**:

| Pair | Lineage A | Lineage B | Pair identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commit** | Titan | Brawler | Frontline: lock a target down and both go in |
| **Root & Void** | Verdant | Hollow | Territory: claim, deny, extract |
| **Hunt** | Thornrunner | Bonespire | Picks: mark a weak point and break it |

A 4-player team can field **at most two complete pairs**, so the draft is
about which two pair-identities you bring, or whether you split pairs for
coverage. *(Proposal: no duplicate lineages within a team.)*

Each Ascendant below lists its **role**, **Stage-by-stage silhouette**,
**kit**, **weak point** and **sound signature**. Pages 120 and 124 say
evolution amplifies capabilities *and* sound signatures. Abilities, values
and names in this file are **proposals** built on the source pages.

---

## Shared Base Form (Levels 1–2)

Every player spawns as the same small humanoid Base Form. Base Forms are
**native creatures of Greyborn**. They occur naturally, like the planet's other
wildlife. The six Ascendant lineages are the natural evolutionary paths open
to them: a Base Form becomes a Titan or a Verdant the way a larva becomes a moth.
*(Decided by the director, 2026-10-02. Their in-world species name is still open.)*

- Kit: light melee combo, dodge, **Root** (channel to capture a node), **Return** (start the trip home).
- At **Level 3** the lineage *awakens* and the player gets the lineage kit.
  Until then, enemies can't tell which lineage an opponent picked.

## Titan (Commit pair): Juggernaut / frontline

- **Silhouette:** L3 hunched stone brute → L10 plated colossus → L20 mountain with a crystal spine.
- **Q · Quake Slam:** ground slam; **staggers** for 0.8 s (the setup for Smash & Roll).
- **E · Bulwark:** frontal guard, −60% damage from the front for 3 s.
- **R · Rampart Charge:** unstoppable charge that carries enemies.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Colossus Step*: 6 s of giant form; every step is a mini-quake.
- **Weak point:** crystal spine on the back. Flanking matters.
- **Sound:** deep footfalls you can hear through walls from Stage 2 on.

## Brawler (Commit pair): Diver / bruiser

- **Silhouette:** L3 wiry ape → L10 knuckle-walking gorilla-beast → L20 armoured berserker.
- **Q · Roll Commit:** armoured roll, unstoppable for its duration. Ends in an uppercut.
- **E · Haymaker:** chargeable punch; full charge knocks back.
- **R · Grapple:** grab and throw a smaller (lower-Stage) target.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Frenzy*: 8 s of attack speed and lifesteal.
- **Weak point:** chest plate, exposed only during Roll Commit's recovery.
- **Sound:** rising snarl that gets louder with Momentum.

## Verdant (Root & Void pair): Territory controller / sustain

- **Silhouette:** L3 sapling-limbed creature → L10 bark-armoured stag-beast → L20 walking grove with hyper-rooted growths.
- **Passive · Deep Root:** captures nodes **2× faster**. Its rooted nodes spread growth to adjacent cells.
- **Q · Bramble Wall:** wall that blocks paths (changes the node graph temporarily).
- **E · Sap Draw:** heal allies over time; costs team SAP.
- **R · Entangle:** root enemies in place for 1.2 s.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Overgrowth*: instantly root every Vulnerable node in a radius.
- **Weak point:** bloom pod on the shoulder.
- **Sound:** creaking wood and rustling, heard by anyone standing on its territory.

## Hollow (Root & Void pair): Void caster / zone denial

- **Silhouette:** L3 thin shade with a hole in the chest → L10 robed void-wraith → L20 floating cathedral of emptiness.
- **Q · Void Rift:** area that slows and drains enemy **Control**.
- **E · Hollow Step:** short-range blink.
- **R · Null Pulse:** silences enemies (no abilities) for 1.5 s.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Event Horizon*: pulls enemies to a central point.
- **Weak point:** core cavity in the torso, a large target from the front.
- **Sound:** an inverted hum; nearby sound briefly drops out.

## Thornrunner (Hunt pair): Stalker / assassin

- **Silhouette:** L3 spiny hound → L10 long-legged insectoid quadruped → L20 bladed horror.
- **Q · Thorn Dash:** fast dash through enemies, applies bleed.
- **E · Mark Prey:** reveals a target and **exposes its weak point** to the whole team for 6 s.
- **R · Barb Volley:** spray of thorns, short range.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Hunt Frenzy*: move faster and see all enemies carrying 200+ cores.
- **Weak point:** abdomen sac.
- **Sound:** skittering. Quietest Ascendant at every Stage.

## Bonespire (Hunt pair): Siege sniper

- **Silhouette:** L3 hunched archer with bone shards → L10 spined ballista-beast → L20 cathedral of bone spikes.
- **Q · Spire Lance:** long-range skillshot; **2× damage on exposed weak points**.
- **E · Ossuary:** spike fence that blocks movement.
- **R · Calcify:** slows target; stacks into a stun.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate:** *Bone Cathedral*: plants itself as a turret that deals siege damage to Hubs and Cores.
- **Weak point:** marrow spire on the back.
- **Sound:** clicking bone. Each lance makes a loud crack that gives away its position.

## Stat profiles (Health · Power · Speed · Control)

The source page charts these four stats from Level 1 to Level 20 (p. 121).
These are proposed endpoints; growth follows an S-curve with most of the gain
from Level 8 to Level 16.

| Lineage | Health L1 → L10 → L20 | Power | Speed | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base Form (L1–2) | 600 | 40 | 100 | 10 |
| Titan | 600 → 2,600 → 5,200 | 40 → 100 → 180 | 100 → 90 → 85 | 10 → 40 → 70 |
| Brawler | 600 → 2,000 → 3,800 | 40 → 130 → 240 | 100 → 105 → 110 | 10 → 30 → 50 |
| Verdant | 600 → 1,800 → 3,400 | 40 → 85 → 150 | 100 → 95 → 95 | 10 → 60 → 110 |
| Hollow | 600 → 1,600 → 3,000 | 40 → 115 → 210 | 100 → 100 → 100 | 10 → 55 → 100 |
| Thornrunner | 600 → 1,500 → 2,800 | 40 → 125 → 230 | 100 → 120 → 135 | 10 → 35 → 60 |
| Bonespire | 600 → 1,450 → 2,700 | 40 → 140 → 260 | 100 → 92 → 90 | 10 → 45 → 80 |

- **Speed** is a % of base move speed. Big Ascendants get *relatively* slower,
  except the Thornrunner.
- **Control** scales crowd-control duration *and* node capture/uproot power.
