# 24 — The Three Minds

Greyborn's story is driven by three wills: **the planet** (decided: alive and
aware), **the Murmur** (decided: a hive mind) and, from Season 3, **the
Clamor** (decided: a rival hive mind; name proposed). This chapter defines how
each one thinks, so story, events and Memories stay consistent. All
characterisation here is a proposal.

## At a glance

| | **Greyborn** (the planet) | **The Murmur** | **The Clamor** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | A living world, aware in a slow, deep way | A single mind spread through crystal and the creatures it takes | A roaring swarm-mind spread through burning spore |
| Arrived | Always here | On the Starwound meteorite, long ago | On the second meteorite (Season 3) |
| Wants | To live, heal and keep growing | To reach the Heartwood and make the planet one infected body | To consume. Possibly to catch the Murmur |
| Creates? | **Yes.** It grows new life and new answers | **No.** It can only copy and corrupt | **No.** It can only devour |
| Time sense | Ages; seasons are heartbeats | Patient; centuries mean little | Frantic; everything is now |
| Speaks through | Growth, tremor, instinct | Whispers | Noise: overlapping roaring voices |
| Feels about the Kith | Protective, like a parent who sends children to war because it must | Curious; they're the best material to copy | Indifferent; they're food |
| Weakness | Slow to act; sleeps to heal | Can't invent anything new | Burns itself out between surges |
| Patron events | Planet Pulse | Murmur Surge | Landfall (hostile to both) |

## Greyborn, the planet

**Personality:** ancient, patient, protective, and **in pain**. Since the
Fall, the planet has felt every hurt the Murmur does to it.

- **It loves through growth.** Its way of caring is to grow something: a
  healing bloom, a wall of trees, a new answer.
- **It doesn't understand individuals easily.** It thinks in herds, broods and
  forests. When a single Kith ascends, the planet notices it the way you'd
  notice a single warm day.
- **It makes hard choices.** It calls broods to evolve and fight, knowing many
  will die. It mourns them by taking them back into the soil.
- **It holds back its strongest power.** The Sleeper (Stillheart) is proof
  that it can make things it is afraid of. Why it put that answer to sleep is
  one of the planet's secrets.

**How it should feel to players:** awe and belonging. When the planet acts
(a Planet Pulse, a Heartwood bloom), it should feel like something enormous
noticed you.

## The Murmur

**Personality:** patient, curious, precise, and possibly **lonely**.

- **It learns by copying.** Every creature it takes, it studies. It copied the
  First Answers, the lineages, even Stillheart.
- **It doesn't hate the planet.** It wants to *become* it, or to make the
  planet part of itself, which from the Murmur's view is the same thing.
- **It remembers fleeing.** Season 3's Memory suggests it once fled the Clamor
  across the dark. Its slow spread on Greyborn may be not conquest but **hiding**.
- **It is many and one.** Blighted creatures move in perfect step; the
  Blightborn hear one voice in many whispers.

**How it should feel to players:** unsettling beauty. Playing Blightborn
should feel like being part of something vast and coordinated, not like being evil ([10](10-world.md#tone-guide)).

## The Clamor

**Personality:** ravenous, chaotic, loud, **without patience or curiosity**.

- **It doesn't copy or plan.** It devours whatever it touches, then surges on.
- **It hunts.** It came to Greyborn following something, most likely the Murmur.
- **It cannot be bargained with, or turned.** No rallying or infecting Clamor creatures ([22](22-season-3.md)).

**How it should feel to players:** a storm. Something both sides drop
everything to deal with.

## How the three relate

```
            GREYBORN (the planet)
           /       creates        \
   fights │                        │ is eaten by
          ▼                        ▼
     THE MURMUR ◄────── hunts ──── THE CLAMOR
   copies the planet           devours everything
```

- **Planet vs. Murmur:** the core war. Every match.
- **Clamor vs. both:** a common threat. It raises the question of whether the
  planet and the Murmur could ever stand together, and what the Murmur would be
  if it stopped hiding.
- **Long-term arc (decided, 2026-10-02):** the Murmur slowly changes by
  copying the planet's creatures, and it **learns to create**. Its first
  creation is the Glass Flower (Season 4), and the turning point of the story
  follows in Season 5 ([47](47-season-5.md)). This also changes one row of the
  table above: the Murmur starts as "can't create" and grows out of it.

## Rules for writers and designers

1. **None of the three ever speaks in words.** All communication is growth,
   sound, behaviour and Memory visions ([13](13-legends.md)).
2. **The planet is never shown as all-powerful.** It's vast but slow, and it
   can lose regions.
3. **The Murmur is never shown as simply evil.** It has reasons, even if they're alien.
4. **The Clamor is never explained fully.** It stays a force of nature from outside.
5. **Gameplay stays a mirror.** No story beat ever gives one playable side a
   mechanical advantage over the other.

## How each mind is shown on screen

None of the three minds ever speaks, so each has a visual and audio language.

| Mind | When it acts, players see… | …and hear |
|---|---|---|
| **The planet** | Roots reaching, ground rising, flowers opening, an amber glow under the soil | A deep exhale, a slow heartbeat, wood creaking |
| **The Murmur** | Blight spreading in fine lines, creatures turning together, a cyan-violet pulse | Whispers in harmony, a ringing tone |
| **The Clamor** | Red streaks in the sky, crust spreading, steam and burning | Overlapping roars and shouts, crackling fire |

## Moments where the minds touch

These are the few times in the story when two minds meet directly. They're
the emotional peaks, and each one should be staged with care.

1. **The Fall:** the Murmur's stone strikes the planet. Pain on one side, hope on the other.
2. **The Hushed:** the Murmur studies the First Answers. The first time it sees the planet's best work.
3. **The Glass Flower:** the planet's roots touch the flower and flinch at something foreign.
4. **Braided ground:** roots and crystal grow into each other against the Clamor.
5. **The fever:** the planet burns the braid without knowing what it destroys.
6. **The Last Braid:** neither mind touches it.

## The minds as characters

Even without words, each mind needs a character arc:

- **The planet** goes from *wounded and defensive* to *curious* (Season 4–5)
  to *allied* (Season 6) to *regretful* (Season 7, a Memory shows it missing the crystal).
- **The Murmur** goes from *hiding* to *creating* to *hoping* to *betrayed*.
- **The Clamor** has no arc. It's a storm. That's what makes it frightening.
