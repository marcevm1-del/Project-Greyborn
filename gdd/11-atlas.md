# 11 — Atlas of Greyborn

> Names **Starwound, the Murmur, Wildborn, Blightborn, Heartwood** were
> approved by the director (2026-10-02). Everything else here is a proposal.

## The shape of the world

Greyborn has one vast, broken landmass, the **Greyreach**, surrounded by a
cold grey ocean, the **Stillsea**.

- The meteorite struck the far north. The **Starwound** crater is the
  Murmur's stronghold, and Blight spreads outward from it like cracks in glass.
- On the **exact opposite side of the world** lies the **Heartwood**, the place
  where the planet's awareness is deepest. The Murmur's goal is to reach it.
- Between them, every region sits somewhere on an **infection gradient**, from
  untouched to almost lost. This gives each region a clear place in the war
  and a natural basis for a planet-wide war map later (open question 14).

```
                       ✶ STARWOUND (north)
                 Shattered Coast   Glasswaste
          Spirecliffs          Cinderveil
     Rimewastes     Ashen Steppe      Bone Flats
          Hollow Mire          Underroot (beneath everything)
                 Rootwilds
                       ♥ HEARTWOOD (south)
        ~ ~ ~ the Stillsea surrounds the Greyreach ~ ~ ~
```

### The body of the planet

Each region is a part of Greyborn's body. This tells artists, sound designers
and writers what the region *feels* like.

| Region | Part of the planet | Murmur presence |
|---|---|---|
| Heartwood | Heart | None (it would be the end of the war) |
| Rootwilds | Lungs | Low |
| Underroot | Nerves | Pockets everywhere |
| Ashen Steppe | Skin | Medium |
| Hollow Mire | Gut | Medium |
| Spirecliffs | Spine | Medium |
| Bone Flats | Memory | Medium–high |
| Rimewastes | Sleep | Low but rising |
| Cinderveil | Fever | High |
| Shattered Coast | Wound edge | Very high |
| Glasswaste | Scar tissue (lost to Blight) | Total |
| Starwound | The wound itself | Origin |

## Regions

### ♥ The Heartwood
A forest of trees so old and vast that their roots are hills. SAP pools
openly in amber lakes. The air hums with the planet's awareness, and
creatures here act strangely calm, as if being watched over.
- **Hazards:** the grasping ground (roots that snare intruders).
- **Wildlife:** Mossback Grazers, Sapback Aphids, Rootwalkers. No infected creatures can survive here for long.
- **Landmark:** **The First Root**, a single root as wide as a mountain, said to reach the planet's core.
- **Match use:** each match map holds a *Heartwood grove*, a fragment of this power (see [10](10-world.md#crater-zones-and-heartwood-groves)).

### Rootwilds (lungs)
Endless living forest that breathes. The canopy rises and falls with a slow
rhythm, and mist exhales from it at dawn.
- **Hazards:** dense undergrowth (short sightlines), Weavemother webs.
- **Wildlife:** Glimmerfoxes, Mossback Grazers, Threadlings, Rootworms, Sapdrinkers.
- **Landmark:** **The Breathing Canopy**, a valley where the whole forest rises and falls like a chest.
- **Map feel:** many small nodes, tight paths, ambush play. Verdant country.

### Underroot (nerves)
A network of caves beneath the whole Greyreach, lit by glowing roots and
Lantern Lizards. The planet's nerves run through it. Signals travel through
the walls as faint pulses of light.
- **Hazards:** darkness, collapsing tunnels, Glasslegs.
- **Wildlife:** Burrowtusks, Lantern Lizards, Glasslegs, the Eightfold Matron, the Sixfold Wyrm.
- **Murmur:** Blight creeps through the tunnels in hidden pockets. It's the Murmur's fastest road.
- **Map feel:** tunnels connecting surface nodes; flanking routes under the map.

### Ashen Steppe (skin)
Wind-swept grey grassland under a pale sky. Fine ash drifts constantly. Huge
herds migrate across it.
- **Hazards:** ashfall storms (visibility drops), stampedes.
- **Wildlife:** Ashfangs, Spurlarks, Gravel Skinks, Stiltwalkers, Ember Moths, the Greyback Colossus, the Hive Colossus.
- **Landmark:** **The Grey Migration**, a river of herds that crosses the steppe and can block a battle for a full minute.
- **Map feel:** large nodes, long sightlines, Bonespire heaven, open teamfights.

### Hollow Mire (gut)
A fog-drowned swamp where sound dies. Slow, warm and full of things that eat
whatever falls in.
- **Hazards:** deep mud (−20% speed), sound damping, Hexmaws.
- **Wildlife:** Duskmanes, Strider Cranes, Hollowmonks, Hexmaws, Tendril Crawlers, Mire Scuttlers, Hush Cicadas.
- **Landmark:** **The Swallowing Pool**, a bottomless SAP-black pool that slowly absorbs anything that dies nearby.
- **Map feel:** stealth, ambush, sound-signature play. Hollow country.

### Spirecliffs (spine)
Towering columns of grey rock with ledges, bridges and sheer drops. The
planet's spine breaking through the skin.
- **Hazards:** falls, wind gusts, Sixhorn Rams that knock players off ledges.
- **Wildlife:** Stonehide Oxen, Knucklebrutes, Sixhorn Rams, Rimeback Stags, Thornwasps.
- **Landmark:** **The Vertebrae**, a line of mountain-peaks shaped like a spine, visible from half the Greyreach.
- **Map feel:** vertical, chokepoints, high ground. Titan and Brawler country.

### Bone Flats (memory)
A white salt plain covered in the fossil bones of creatures far larger than
any alive today. The planet remembers everything that ever lived on it.
- **Hazards:** salt glare (reduced vision at noon), bone fields that slow movement.
- **Wildlife:** Marrowhounds, Bonewrights, Trundlebacks, Bone Harvestmen, Siege Beetles, Marrow Mantises.
- **Murmur:** the Murmur hunts here for old bones to copy. Blight spreads along the fossils.
- **Landmark:** **The Elder Ribs**, a fossil ribcage the size of a city. Some say it belonged to the first Titan.
- **Map feel:** open with clusters of bone cover. Bonespire country.

### Rimewastes (sleep)
A frozen plateau in the west where the planet "sleeps". Life here moves
slowly and dreams. The Murmur hasn't bothered with it yet, but frost-Blight
has begun to appear.
- **Hazards:** cold (slow Health regeneration), thin ice over deep water, whiteout.
- **Wildlife:** Rimeback Stags, Stiltwalkers, Glasslegs (in ice caves), proposed new cold-adapted variants.
- **Landmark:** **The Sleeper**, an enormous creature frozen under the ice. Is it alive?
- **Map feel:** slippery footing, slow fights, breakable ice floors.

### Cinderveil (fever)
Volcanic highlands where the meteorite's heat woke old volcanoes. The planet
is running a fever, trying to burn the infection out. Lava runs between
blackened forests.
- **Hazards:** lava rivers, eruptions on a timer, heat haze.
- **Wildlife:** heat-adapted Ashfangs and Siege Beetles. Ember Moths swarm here.
- **Murmur:** Blight here is half-melted. Both sides suffer.
- **Landmark:** **The Fevermouth**, a volcano that erupts when Blight comes too close.
- **Map feel:** fast, dangerous, timed hazards. This matches the source art's lava-rock Stage 3 form.

### Shattered Coast (wound edge)
Where meteor fragments fell into the sea. The cliffs are studded with
glowing shards, and the Stillsea's waves glitter cyan-violet with Blight.
- **Hazards:** shard storms, tidal surges.
- **Wildlife:** mostly Blighted variants; Mire Scuttlers live along the tideline.
- **Landmark:** **The Shard Reef**, a reef of meteor glass rising out of the sea, singing in the wind.
- **Map feel:** half-blighted from the start. A front line.

### Glasswaste (scar tissue)
Land already lost to the Murmur. Forest turned to black glass, creatures
frozen mid-step inside it. Whispering is constant.
- **Hazards:** sharp ground (damage over time for Wildborn), whispers that reveal positions.
- **Wildlife:** only Blighted variants: Hushed Strays, Glassback Grazers, Glass Weavers.
- **Landmark:** **The Frozen Herd**, an entire migration caught in glass mid-gallop.
- **Map feel:** Blightborn home ground. Every map's *Starwound crater* zone draws from here.

### ✶ The Starwound
The crater itself, miles wide. At its centre lies the meteorite, still warm,
pulsing with the Murmur's will. It isn't a place anyone visits casually.
- **Landmark:** **The Seed**, the meteorite's core, around which the Murmur's thoughts gather.
- **Use:** for story moments, seasonal events and a possible endgame map. Not a standard match map.

## Proposed launch match maps

| Map | Regions | Starwound crater zone | Heartwood grove | Identity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ashfall Crossing** | Ashen Steppe + Underroot | East edge | West edge | Open teamfights, migration events, tunnels for flanks |
| **The Elder Ribs** | Bone Flats | Inside the fossil ribcage | Old root-knot outside it | Siege play around a central bone fortress |
| **Breathing Canopy** | Rootwilds + Hollow Mire | Fallen shard in the mire | The Breathing valley | Tight ambush map, stealth and nodes |

Later maps: **Fevermouth** (Cinderveil, eruption timer), **The Sleeper**
(Rimewastes, breakable ice) and **Shard Reef** (Shattered Coast, tides).
