## From the atlas

Where meteor fragments fell into the sea. The cliffs are studded with
glowing shards, and the Stillsea's waves glitter cyan-violet with Blight.
- **Hazards:** shard storms, tidal surges.
- **Wildlife:** mostly Blighted variants; Mire Scuttlers live along the tideline.
- **Landmark:** **The Shard Reef**, a reef of meteor glass rising out of the sea, singing in the wind.
- **Map feel:** half-blighted from the start. A front line.
