## From the atlas

Land already lost to the Murmur. Forest turned to black glass, creatures
frozen mid-step inside it. Whispering is constant.
- **Hazards:** sharp ground (damage over time for Wildborn), whispers that reveal positions.
- **Wildlife:** only Blighted variants: Hushed [[Strays]], Glassback Grazers, Glass Weavers.
- **Landmark:** **The Frozen Herd**, an entire migration caught in glass mid-gallop.
- **Map feel:** Blightborn home ground. Every map's *Starwound crater* zone draws from here.
