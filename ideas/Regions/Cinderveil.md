## From the atlas

Volcanic highlands where the meteorite's heat woke old volcanoes. The planet
is running a fever, trying to burn the infection out. Lava runs between
blackened forests.
- **Hazards:** lava rivers, eruptions on a timer, heat haze.
- **Wildlife:** heat-adapted Ashfangs and Siege Beetles. Ember Moths swarm here.
- **Murmur:** Blight here is half-melted. Both sides suffer.
- **Landmark:** **The Fevermouth**, a volcano that erupts when Blight comes too close.
- **Map feel:** fast, dangerous, timed hazards. This matches the source art's lava-rock Stage 3 form.
