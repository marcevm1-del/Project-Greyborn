# 27 — Modes

The core of Greyborn is the 4v4 match from the source pages. This chapter
proposes the full set of modes around it, each rooted in the world. All
modes beyond the core 4v4 are proposals.

## Mode list

| Mode | Players | Status | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Answering** (core) | 4v4 | Core (source pages) | The full game: evolution, territory, Synergies |
| **The Answering (Ranked)** | 4v4 | Proposal | Competitive ladder and War Map community goals |
| **Brood Skirmish** | 2v2 | Proposal | Short matches built around **one pair per team** |
| **Apex Hunt** | 4v4 | Proposal | Casual: race to bring down Greyborn's great beasts |
| **The Truce** | 4-player co-op | Proposal (Season 3+) | Wildborn and Blightborn together against the Clamor |
| **Season finale modes** | 4v4 | Proposal | Limited-time story events (e.g. The Glass Range Walks) |
| **The Den (training)** | Solo / AI | Proposal | Practice against AI broods, combo training |
| **Custom & tournament** | 4v4 | Proposal | Private matches with fixed rules for competitions |

## The Answering (Ranked)

- Same rules as the core match ([06](06-match-flow.md)).
- **Draft:** the full pick-and-hide draft (lineages hidden until Level 3).
- **Sides:** assigned per match, and balanced across a player's history so
  everyone plays both sides about equally.
- **Ranks** named after the lineage mastery language: *Seedling*, *Sapling*,
  *Rooted*, *Old Growth*, *First Answer* (top rank).
- **War Map:** ranked and unranked matches both earn front points; ranked matches don't count extra (to keep the war open to everyone).

## Brood Skirmish (2v2)

The pair system is the heart of Greyborn, so this mode isolates it.

- **Each team is exactly one pair** (Titan + Brawler, Verdant + Hollow,
  Thornrunner + Bonespire), or Stillheart + any lineage.
- **Small maps** (about 160 × 160 m), 10 nodes, 1 Hub, 1 Enemy Core each.
- **Faster evolution:** EXP ×2, so Synergies unlock in about 8 minutes.
- **10-minute matches**, first to 70% Territorial Influence or most at time.
- **Why:** it teaches pairs, it's quick to play with one friend, and it
  feeds Pair Bonds ([26](26-collection.md#pair-bonds)).

## Apex Hunt (casual 4v4)

- A large map with **several apex creatures** awake at once (Greyback
  Colossus, Hive Colossus, Eightfold Matron, Sixfold Wyrm).
- Teams race to bring down apexes. Each apex is worth points; the last hit
  wins its points, so teams have to fight each other at the kill.
- No base conversion: cores convert instantly, so the mode is fast and
  forgiving. Good for new players and for showing off the wildlife.

## The Truce (co-op, Season 3+)

The lore question from [22](22-season-3.md#open-threads-for-season-4-and-beyond)
(*could the planet and the Murmur ever stand together?*), made playable.

- **4 players:** two **Wildborn** and two **Blightborn** on the same team. Their
  roots and Blight both count as "held ground".
- **Enemy:** the Clamor. Waves of Shriekers, Scald Hulks, Ashmouths and Spore
  Kites, with Landfalls getting bigger each wave.
- **Objective:** hold the map's territory for 15 minutes against spreading Scald.
- **Story beat:** at the end, the Murmur and the planet both "notice" what
  happened. It's a Memory unlocked in the Codex, and seeds the long-term
  question of whether the war could ever change ([24](24-the-three-minds.md#how-the-three-relate)).
- **Fairness:** it's co-op, so the mirror rule isn't at risk.

## The Den (training)

- **Practice range:** every lineage, at any Stage, with dummies that show weak
  points, Flare-style hit feedback and damage numbers.
- **Synergy drills:** practise Smash & Roll timing (1.5 s window), Co-Stalk
  (4 s window) and Void Garden placement with an AI partner.
- **AI broods:** full 4v4 against bots, at three difficulties.

## Custom & tournament

- Private matches with every rule exposed: phase timings, conversion times,
  wildlife on/off, patron events on/off, weather fixed.
- **Tournament preset:** fixed weather (Clear), patron events **off**
  (comeback mechanics are unpopular in high-level competition; to be tested),
  draft with bans (1 ban per team).
