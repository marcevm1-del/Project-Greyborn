# 19 — Sound & Music of Greyborn

Greyborn has **no words**: no speech, no writing (decided). Sound carries
much of the storytelling, and the source pages make **sound signatures** a
gameplay system. This chapter sets the audio identity. All content is a proposal.

## The two voices of the world

| | The planet (Wildborn) | The Murmur (Blightborn) |
|---|---|---|
| Core sound | **Breath, wood and stone**: creaks, low rumbles, wind through leaves, heartbeats deep underground | **Glass and whisper**: chimes, ringing tones, layered whispering that's slightly out of sync |
| Musical palette | Low strings, frame drums, wooden flutes, throat-singing drones, bowed bass, rain sticks | Glass harmonica, bowed metal, crystal bowls, choirs reversed and pitch-shifted, granular textures |
| Rhythm | Slow and organic, like breathing (the Rootwilds' 90 s cycle) | Precise and synchronised, like a single will |
| Silence means… | Rest and safety | Danger: where the Murmur is strongest, nature goes quiet ([15](15-ecology.md#the-murmur-breaks-the-food-web)) |

**Rule:** a player with their eyes closed should be able to tell whether
they're in Wildborn or Blightborn territory.

## Gameplay sound (must be readable)

These are **information**, not decoration, and they have mixing priority.

| Sound | Information it carries |
|---|---|
| **Lineage sound signature** | Which lineage is near, and how big. Louder and deeper with each Stage ([03](03-evolution-system.md)) |
| **Carry signature** | A player carrying 150+ cores has an audible aura; 300+ is louder and on the enemy minimap |
| **Stage transformation** | A map-wide tremor (Wildborn) or ringing chime with whispers (Blightborn), which also tells you the direction |
| **Weak point break** | A sharp crack (Wildborn) or shatter (Blightborn) |
| **Synergy triggers** | Each of the three pair Synergies has a unique, short sting, so both teams know it happened |
| **Node captured / lost** | A low root-pulse (yours) or a dissonant chord (theirs) |
| **Tension at 75+** | A rising drone in that region, warning that a global event is near |
| **Patron event** | **Planet Pulse:** a huge deep exhale from under the ground. **Murmur Surge:** a sudden wall of whispering voices |
| **Wildlife alarms** | Strider Crane cries, herd stampedes, Thornwasp buzz: readable signals of nearby fighting |

**Accessibility:** every gameplay sound has an optional **visual indicator**
(directional ring on screen), so deaf and hard-of-hearing players get the same information.

## Music

- **Adaptive by match phase** ([06](06-match-flow.md)):
  1. *Stable Flow:* ambient, sparse, mostly region soundscape.
  2. *Pre-Aggro Resource Control:* rhythm enters (drums or chimes, depending on side).
  3. *Resource Stage:* full ensemble during fights; drops back between them.
  4. *Hunt:* the two musical palettes **collide**: organic and glass themes
     playing against each other.
- **Side-aware:** the player hears their own side's palette in the lead and
  the enemy's as a threat underneath.
- **Main theme:** one melody played two ways: on wooden flute and low strings
  (the planet), and on glass harmonica with whispering voices (the Murmur).
  The Murmur's version is the same tune, *copied and slightly wrong*, which
  matches its nature in the lore.

## Each lineage's voice in depth

| Lineage | Footsteps | Breathing | Signature sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Titan** | Deep, grinding stone that shakes nearby debris | Slow, like wind through a cave | The **Quake Slam**: a crack of rock and a rolling rumble |
| **Brawler** | Quick knuckle-thumps, uneven | Fast, snorting | A growl that rises with Momentum |
| **Verdant** | Soft, with the rustle of leaves | Long and calm | Creaking wood and the patter of falling petals |
| **Hollow** | None: it glides | Barely audible, a hollow draw of air | The sound of other sounds dimming around it |
| **Thornrunner** | Fast skittering clicks | Short, panting | A trilling click when it Marks prey |
| **Bonespire** | Measured, with a rattle of bones | Slow, with a whistle through bone | The crack of a Spire Lance, heard across the map |
| **Stillheart** | Very soft, with the crunch of frost | Slow, visible as frost in the air | Its heartbeat, slow when calm, racing in a fight |

**Blightborn versions** keep the same rhythm and pitch, so the sound still
tells you the lineage and size, but the material changes: stone becomes ringing
glass, wood becomes chimes, and every breath has a whisper underneath.

## The main theme

The main theme is a slow, simple melody of eight notes, easy to hum.

- **The planet's version** opens on a solo wooden flute over a deep drone,
  then adds low strings and frame drums. It sounds old, warm and patient.
- **The Murmur's version** plays the same eight notes on glass harmonica, with
  whispering voices underneath. One note is **slightly flat**: a copy, not
  quite right. It sounds beautiful and a little wrong.
- **Season 5's turning point** ([47](47-season-5.md)) adds a third version: the
  Murmur's **own** tune, the hum of the Hushed Kith. It uses the same instruments
  as the Murmur's theme but a new melody. For the first time, the Murmur isn't
  copying the planet's music.
- **Season 6's Truce** ([49](49-season-6.md)) plays the planet's and the
  Murmur's themes **together**, in harmony, for the first time.
- **Season 7's Unbraiding** ([51](51-season-7.md)) pulls them apart again. The
  harmony breaks mid-phrase.

The music tells the story of the two minds across the seasons, without a single word.

## Silence as design

Greyborn uses silence on purpose:

- **Before a big moment:** half a second of silence before a Stage 3
  transformation, a Base Heart falling, or a patron event.
- **The Hollow Mire and Hush Cicadas:** silence is part of the map.
- **The Glasswaste:** the absence of birds and insects tells the player the
  Murmur has won here ([15](15-ecology.md#the-murmur-breaks-the-food-web)).
- **The end of a match:** the losing team's final moments fade to a single heartbeat.

## Audio across the seasons

| Season | Audio change |
|---|---|
| 1 · The Hush Wakes | A low glass hum on the horizon of every map, growing each week |
| 2 · The Sleeper Stirs | The Sleeper's heartbeat under every frozen surface |
| 3 · The Second Light | The Clamor's roar enters the mix: the first sound that is neither wood nor glass |
| 4 · The Glass Flower | A single high glass tone near the flower that matches nothing else |
| 5 · The First Made Thing | The Hushed Kith's hum |
| 6 · The Roaring Comes | Both themes in harmony; the Clamor's roar at its loudest |
| 7 · The Unbraiding | The harmony breaks; the hum falls silent |
