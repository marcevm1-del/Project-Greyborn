# 04 — Progression & Economy

Greyborn has **four progression tracks.** Each answers a different player question.

| Track | Player question | Permanence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grey Arts** (skill tree) | "How good is *my own* kit?" | Permanent | Dross, spent at Lanterns |
| **Echoes** | "Where can I go?" | Permanent | Prism Hearts (bosses) |
| **Residue** | "What am I becoming?" | Permanent (removable at great cost) | Crossing Brand-tier Stain |
| **Relics** | "How do I want to play?" | Swappable | Exploration, quests |

## Currency: Dross

- Dross is the grey dust left behind when Hue leaves a body. Every enemy drops some.
- Uses: Grey Arts, Mend upgrades, merchant goods, Residue removal.
- Lost on death until recovered (see [03](03-core-gameplay.md#death)).
- Rough economy target: one region (~2–3 hrs) earns enough for 4–6 Grey Arts if
  the player doesn't die often.

## Grey Arts (the Greyborn's own skill tree)

Three branches, 8 nodes each (24 total), bought at any Lantern. Costs scale
by tier (Tier 1: 300, Tier 2: 900, Tier 3: 2,500, Capstone: 6,000 Dross).

**Hollow** (base combat):
- Blade string +1 hit · Charged grey slash · Parry window +2 frames ·
  Air parry · Dodge-cancel from blade · Mend heals over time ·
  Riposte damage +50% · **Capstone: Null Edge**: the blade does 2× damage
  while *no* Hue is held. This is the pure-Grey build-defining node.

**Vessel** (holding Hues):
- Saturation fade −30% · Rip range +50% · Rip heals 10% HP ·
  Swap Vessels instantly refunds 10 Saturation · Blend Arts cost −20% ·
  2nd Vessel upgrade (also story-gated) · Release creates a small burst ·
  **Capstone: Prism Vessel**: Clashing no longer doubles Stain.

**Pallor** (resisting Stain):
- Stain from holding −25% · Stain drains 2× faster · Mark-tier has no downside ·
  Brand Vice effects shortened · Damage-taken Stain −50% ·
  Overcolour lasts 20 s · Stain floor from Residue −2 per point ·
  **Capstone: Grey Will**: once per Lantern, the first Husking is
  prevented and Stain drops to 66.

Full respec costs a rare item (Prismatic Ash, 6 in the game).

## Relics (build-crafting)

- Equippable trinkets. **Relic slots:** 2 at start, up to 5.
- ~40 relics at launch. Each fits one of three roles:
  - **Hue-tuning:** "Kindle leaves a second line of fire"; "Afterimage lasts 2 s longer".
  - **Rule-bending:** "You can rip non-Flared enemies below 10% HP, but Rip costs 10 HP".
  - **Residue-feeding:** "+X power per point of Violet Residue". These reward
    players who commit to a colour.
- Sample relics:
  - *Cracked Kiln-Seal*: Crimson Stain cannot exceed 90, but Crimson Saturation fades 2× faster.
  - *Oriel's Bandage*: Mend also drains 15 Stain.
  - *Choir Bell*: White (unparryable) tells are also shown 10 frames earlier.
  - *Husk Locket*: on being Husked, revive once with 30% HP (single use, then breaks).

## Mend & Vessel upgrades

- **Mend charges:** 3 → 6 via *Grey Lantern Wicks* (6 hidden in the world).
- **Mend potency:** upgraded by the Lanternwick apothecary (3 tiers, Dross).
- **Vessel 2:** story-gated (end of act 1).
- **Vessel 3:** optional. Requires the *Prism Shard* side-quest **and** at
  least 6 total Residue, so the most powerful build tool is tied to having
  already stained yourself.

## Pacing target (main path)

| Hour | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Tutorial region (Ashfall Orphanage) — base kit, first Rip, first Lantern |
| 1–4 | First region + Prism Heart #1 (player choice of 2 starting regions) |
| ~6 | 2nd Vessel, Blends unlocked |
| 6–14 | Regions 2–4 in any order (gated by Echoes) |
| ~14 | Reveal (after 4th Prism Heart) |
| 14–20 | Regions 5–6, Vessel 3 optional |
| 20–25 | White Cathedral & ending |

## Difficulty & accessibility

- Default difficulty is tuned "hard but fair". There is no easy *mode*; instead
  there is an **Assist menu** (toggles that don't affect endings):
  - Parry window ×1.5 / ×2
  - Saturation fade off
  - Dross not dropped on death
  - Damage taken 50% / 75%
  - Colour-blind palettes (3 presets) + symbol overlay on every Hue (shape per Hue)
- **Accessibility note:** because colour carries meaning, *every* Hue also has a
  unique **shape glyph** and **sound signature**. This is a hard requirement,
  not optional polish.
