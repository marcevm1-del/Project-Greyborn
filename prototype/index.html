Rules in this build

  • Flare & Rip. Hits and parries fill an enemy's Flare bar. When it is full the enemy glows and staggers. Press E to rip its Hue into your Vessel.
  • ▲ Crimson (Cinder Hounds). Art: Kindle, a burning 3-hit lunge. Dodge becomes a flame-dash. Parry explodes.
  • ◆ Azure (Drowned Knights). Art: Undertow, a wave that pulls enemies in. Dodge becomes a long slide. Parry raises a 2-hit tide-shield.
  • ○ Bleached Wardens can't be ripped. A white glyph means the attack can't be parried, so dodge it.
  • Saturation fades 1 per second and drains with use. At 0 the Hue fades for free.
  • Stain rises while you hold a Hue, use its Art, or take hits. Mark (34+) adds damage. Brand (67+) adds more damage plus a Vice. At 100 you get Overcolour for 10 s, then you are Husked.
  • Residue. Each time Stain crosses 67 you gain a permanent point: Crimson gives +4% blade damage, Azure gives −3% damage taken. Each point also raises that Hue's Stain floor by 6.
  • Death drops your Dross as a grey shade. Touch it to recover the Dross.
