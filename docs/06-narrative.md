# 06 — Narrative

## The protagonist

An unnamed Greyborn of about 17, raised in the Ashfall Orphanage. NPCs call
them **"Grey"**. They speak rarely, through short dialogue choices. Their
appearance is androgynous and greyscale by default, and it changes visibly
with Residue (colour creeping from the hands up to the eyes).

**Character arc:** *from "I am nothing" to deciding what to be.* The Greyborn is
offered three identities: an empty vessel (the Church), a thief of colour (the
Remnants) or a person (the Lanternfolk). The ending is the player's answer.

## Key characters

| Character | Role | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| **Oriel** | Lanternfolk elder, mentor | Partially Husked; hides it. Dies or is Husked in act 3 depending on player choices. |
| **Tamsin Reed** | Fellow Greyborn escapee, rival-friend | Takes Hues recklessly to "keep up" with you. Can become a Husk (and a boss) if their questline is ignored. |
| **Brother Callum** | Pale Church inquisitor hunting you | A sincere believer who slowly doubts. He can be turned into an ally for the finale. |
| **Forgemaster Hal** | Crimson Remnant | Respects strength. He becomes a merchant or an enemy depending on your Crimson Residue. |
| **The Gardener** | Verdant guardian | The one guardian who can be spared. Sparing him changes the ending options. |
| **Matriarch Ysolde Vane** | Final antagonist | Remembers the Spectrum Age's wars. Believes she is saving the world from itself. |

## Three-act structure

**Act 1: Escape (hours 0–6)**
Flee the Orphanage, find Lanternwick and restore the first Prism Heart. You
learn that restoring Hearts brings the land back to life. You gain the 2nd Vessel.
*Theme: hope.*

**Act 2: Restore (hours 6–14)**
Restore Hearts 2–4 in any order. Lanternwick grows. Tamsin's questline unfolds.
Callum pursues you. Hints build up: the Church never seems to *stop* you from
reaching a Heart.
*Theme: power and its cost.*

**The Reveal (after Heart #4)**
Callum (or a found Choir hymnal, if Callum is dead) reveals the plan: the
Greyborn are the Hymn's vessels, and every Heart you restore marks you as
the finished offering. Oriel admits he knew.
*The player can keep going or walk straight to the Cathedral now (an early, harder ending path).*

**Act 3: Choose (hours 14–25)**
Restore the remaining Hearts (or don't). Confront Tamsin. Enter the White
Cathedral. Face Ysolde.
*Theme: identity.*

## Endings

The ending is decided by three things: **total Residue**, **Hearts restored**
and **key choices** (Gardener spared, Tamsin saved, Callum turned).
The calculation is transparent: Oriel comments on your state at every Lantern
visit, so endings never feel arbitrary.

| Ending | Requirements | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **White Hymn** (bad) | Any Hue's Residue = 5, *or* Husked in the final fight | You become the offering. The world bleaches forever. Ysolde weeps, unsure whether she won. |
| **Prism Crown** | 6 Hearts, total Residue ≥ 12 | You absorb the Hymn and become a living Prism, a colour-god. Colour returns, but everyone's Hue is now *yours* to grant. Ambiguous/tyrannical. |
| **Grey Dawn** (true) | 6 Hearts, total Residue ≤ 6, Tamsin saved, Callum turned | You break the Hymn by *refusing* to be filled: you release every Hue back into the world and stay Grey. Colour returns freely. Greyborn are no longer born. |
| **Ember Before Dark** (early) | Enter the Cathedral after the Reveal with only 4 Hearts | Harder final fight (Ysolde has the remaining 2 Hues). The world stays mostly grey, but the Church falls. Bittersweet. |
| **Gardener's Peace** (secret) | Gardener spared + Grey Dawn requirements + hueless final phase | Extended epilogue: the Pall regrows naturally and Oriel lives. |

## Storytelling methods

- **Environmental:** the colour-restoration changes are the main narrative reward.
- **Item lore:** every Wisp, relic and key item carries 2–4 lines of text.
- **Lantern conversations:** short, optional, state-reactive talks with Oriel and Lanternfolk.
- **Memory Wisps:** ripping a Prime Hue from a guardian plays a 20–40 s
  playable memory of that guardian's life before the Bleaching.
- **No long cutscenes.** The longest is under 2 minutes (the Reveal).

## Writing guidelines

- Speak in images, not exposition. ("The kiln remembers every hand that fed it.")
- Never call the Church "evil" in narration. Let the player judge.
- Each Hue has a **voice**: Crimson characters speak in short, hot sentences;
  Azure in long, slow ones; Violet in questions.
