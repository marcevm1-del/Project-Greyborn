# Hollow Mire

![[region-hollow-mire.jpg]]

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| Ground | Deep mud (Spec: −20% speed), reed beds, Weepwillow roots that form walkways 2 m wide |

## From the atlas

A fog-drowned swamp where sound dies. Slow, warm and full of things that eat
whatever falls in.
- **Hazards:** deep mud (−20% speed), sound damping, Hexmaws.
- **Wildlife:** Duskmanes, Strider Cranes, Hollowmonks, Hexmaws, Tendril Crawlers, Mire Scuttlers, Hush Cicadas.
- **Landmark:** **The Swallowing Pool**, a bottomless SAP-black pool that slowly absorbs anything that dies nearby.
- **Map feel:** stealth, ambush, sound-signature play. [[Hollow]] country.
