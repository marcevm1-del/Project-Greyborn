# 40 — The Hushed Answers

The Murmur's six glass copies of the First Answers ([13](13-legends.md#the-hushed-answers)),
designed as **giant encounters** for season finales, the Truce co-op mode, or a
future Starwound mode ([35](35-heart-and-seed.md)). All are proposals.

## Shared rules

- Each Hushed Answer is **enormous** (larger than a Stage 3 Ascendant) and
  attacks only **Wildborn** in PvP finales (as in Season 1), or **everyone** in co-op modes.
- Each has **three crystal hearts** (weak points) that must be broken. The
  rest of its body takes greatly reduced damage. This makes weak-point play
  ([04](04-synergies.md#weak-points)) the core skill against them.
- Each one **copies a lineage's strengths**, so teams with the matching
  First-Answer lineage get a small edge in reading its attacks. In lore,
  the original's heirs know its moves.
- **Phases:** after each heart breaks, it changes behaviour.

## The six

### 1 · The Glass Range (copy of the Mountain That Walked, Titan)
*Season 1 finale ([20](20-season-1.md#finale-mode-the-glass-range-walks)).*
- **Moves:** walks a fixed path. **Glass Quake**: a slam with a huge
  telegraphed ring. **Rampart Wall**: raises a wall of glass across a route.
- **Hearts:** back, shoulders, chest. The chest heart only shows after the other two break.
- **Phase change:** after each heart, it walks faster but its quakes get smaller.
- **Counter-skill:** flanking to reach the back.

### 2 · The Shatterer (copy of the Breaker, Brawler)
- **Moves:** **Shard Roll**: rolls across the arena, leaving a line of glass
  shards that damage over time. **Ringing Blow**: a punch that sends a ring of shards outward.
- **Hearts:** on both fists and on the chest. The fist hearts are only
  exposed for 1 s after each Ringing Blow.
- **Phase change:** loses a fist; rolls more often.
- **Counter-skill:** punishing the moment after its attacks.

### 3 · The Lattice Grove (copy of the Breath, Verdant)
- **Moves:** not one creature but a **slowly walking forest of crystal trees**.
  It **glazes nodes** it passes, turning them Blightborn (or neutral in co-op).
  **Spire Growth**: crystal trees burst up under players.
- **Hearts:** hidden in **three of its trees**. Which three changes after each break.
- **Phase change:** splits into more trees each time.
- **Counter-skill:** territory play: re-capture nodes behind it while hunting for the hearts.

### 4 · The Unspoken (copy of the Quiet, Hollow)
- **Moves:** **invisible**. It can only be found by **sound**: its whispers
  move through the air. **Silence**: a pulse that mutes all player sound and
  removes calls for 5 s.
- **Hearts:** briefly visible as cyan sparks when it attacks.
- **Phase change:** whispers get quieter each phase.
- **Counter-skill:** listening (the game's sound signature system turned
  into the whole fight). Needs strong visual sound indicators for accessibility ([19](19-sound-and-music.md#gameplay-sound-must-be-readable)).

### 5 · The Endless Pursuit (copy of the Hunt That Never Ended, Thornrunner)
- **Moves:** never stops moving. It **circles the map**, and players have to
  intercept it. **Glitter Trail**: damaging dust left behind it.
  **Pounce**: leaps onto the player carrying the most cores.
- **Hearts:** on its abdomen, exposed only when it's Marked (by a Thornrunner) or stunned.
- **Phase change:** runs faster; trail lasts longer.
- **Counter-skill:** prediction and interception, and teamwork to stun it.
- **Special event:** in lore, it runs the opposite way around the world from
  the original Hunt. A rare event could have **both** cross a map at once.

### 6 · The Archive (copy of the Rememberer, Bonespire)
- **Moves:** a cathedral of glass bones. **Recall**: summons glass copies of
  **creatures killed earlier in the match**. **Bone Rain**: long-range shard volleys.
- **Hearts:** inside three glass reliquaries on its body. Each one holds a
  creature copy that's released when the heart breaks.
- **Phase change:** recalls stronger creatures each phase.
- **Counter-skill:** managing adds while focusing hearts. The more wildlife
  died earlier in the match, the harder it is: a link to overhunting ([15](15-ecology.md#overhunting)).

## The six together

A long-term idea: an endgame co-op mode in the **Hushed Watch**, the ring on
the Starwound's floor where all six stand ([35](35-heart-and-seed.md#the-starwound-layers-inward)).
Teams face them one after another, each needing a different skill:
flanking, punishing, territory, listening, intercepting and add control.
It would be the hardest challenge in the game.

## Each encounter, phase by phase

### The Glass Range
1. **Walking:** it advances along its path. Glass Quakes every 15 s. The back heart is reachable by flanking.
2. **Wounded:** after the first heart breaks, it walks faster and raises Rampart Walls to block flankers.
3. **Desperate:** the chest heart opens. It stops walking and slams constantly, but the slams are smaller. A burst-damage window.

### The Shatterer
1. **Rolling:** Shard Rolls across the arena; fist hearts exposed after Ringing Blows.
2. **One-fisted:** after a fist breaks, it rolls more often and its shard lines last longer.
3. **Cornered:** the chest heart is exposed during a long wind-up. Interrupting the wind-up with crowd control is the key.

### The Lattice Grove
1. **Spreading:** it glazes nodes as it walks. Hearts hide in three trees.
2. **Splitting:** after each heart, it splits into more trees and the hearts move.
3. **Last tree:** the final heart sits in a single tree that tries to flee to the nearest glazed node.

### The Unspoken
1. **Listening:** invisible; found only by sound. Silence pulses mute the team.
2. **Fading:** its whispers grow quieter. Teams with Hollow or Stillheart players
   (who read silence well) have an edge in the lore and the design.
3. **Last breath:** it attacks constantly, so its sparks give it away more often. A race.

### The Endless Pursuit
1. **Circling:** it runs the map's edge. Teams must predict its route and set up in its path.
2. **Faster:** its trail of glass dust lasts longer and blocks routes.
3. **Hunting:** it pounces on whoever carries the most cores, so a carrier can be used as bait.

### The Archive
1. **Recalling:** summons glass copies of creatures killed earlier in the match.
2. **Opening:** each broken reliquary releases its stored creature.
3. **The whole Archive:** it recalls everything at once. The team must focus the last heart through a crowd.

## Why each one tests a different skill

The six Hushed Answers are built as a **curriculum**. Each one isolates a
core skill of Greyborn and asks teams to master it:

| Hushed Answer | Skill |
|---|---|
| The Glass Range | Positioning and flanking |
| The Shatterer | Timing and punishing |
| The Lattice Grove | Territory control under pressure |
| The Unspoken | Listening and sound signatures |
| The Endless Pursuit | Prediction and interception |
| The Archive | Managing many enemies while focusing one target |

A team that can beat all six has mastered the game.
