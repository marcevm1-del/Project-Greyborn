# 16 — Launch Maps

Detailed proposals for the three launch maps named in [11 — Atlas](11-atlas.md#proposed-launch-match-maps).
Every value here is a proposal for level design to block out and playtest.

## Rules every map follows

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | About 320 × 320 m of playable space |
| Nodes | About 30 nodes, ~110 cells in total (1–6 cells per node) |
| Resource Hubs | 5: two near each base, one in the centre |
| Enemy Core sites | 3 per team, in that team's half |
| Bases | 1 per team: a **birth-node cluster** (see [12](12-life-on-greyborn.md#the-kith-life-cycle)) |
| Symmetry | **Rotationally symmetric**: both bases get the same routes, distances and resources |
| Starwound crater zone + Heartwood grove | On the **two flanks**, each **equidistant from both bases**. Sides are assigned per match independently of base position, so neither team gets a home-field flank |
| Memory sites | 2–3 per map |
| Return-to-base trip | ≤ 20 s from any Hub on foot (see risk #2 in [08](08-open-questions.md)) |

## Map 1 · Ashfall Crossing

**Regions:** Ashen Steppe above, Underroot below. **Identity:** open
teamfights across grassland, flanks through tunnels, and the Grey Migration
splitting the map in two.

```
                    N  ✶ SHARDFALL HOLLOW (crater zone)
          ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │  ◇      ◇        ║        ◇      ◇        │
 WEST     │ (H)  ◇    ◇     ║ MIGRATION ◇   ◇  (H)  │    EAST
 BASE  ═══│  ◇  ●core    ◇ (H) CENTRE ◇   ●core  ◇   │═══ BASE
          │ (H)  ◇    ◇     ║  ROAD   ◇   ◇   (H)    │
          │  ◇   ●core  ◇   ║    ◇  ●core  ◇   ●core │
          │ ●core ◇  ▓▓ tunnels under the road ▓▓ ◇   │
          └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                    S  ♥ OLD ROOT KNOT (Heartwood grove)
   ◇ node   (H) Resource Hub   ● Enemy Core site   ║ migration road   ▓ Underroot tunnels
```

- **The centre:** a wide plain with the central Hub on a low ash knoll. It's
  the best teamfight space in the launch set.
- **The Grey Migration:** every 6 minutes for 60 s, a river of herds crosses
  the **Migration road** from north to south. Nobody can cross the road on the
  surface while it passes, so the **Underroot tunnels** become the only way
  through. Fights in the tunnels are short and brutal.
- **Shardfall Hollow (north, crater zone):** a field of fallen meteor shards with Starshard veins and shard storms.
- **Old Root Knot (south, Heartwood grove):** a twisted root-hill with SAP springs and grasping ground.
- **Memory sites:**
  1. *The Hunt Passes*: a rise on the north ridge where the Hunt That Never Ended can be glimpsed.
  2. *The Sky Breaks*: an impact scar in Shardfall Hollow.
- **Wildlife:** Ashfang packs (south plains), Spurlarks, Gravel Skinks, Stiltwalkers
  (players can hide under them), Clashhorn Beetles, Ember Moths at dusk, Lantern Lizards in the tunnels.
- **Apex:** the **Greyback Colossus** joins the Migration in Phase 3. Its back is a moving node that crosses the whole map.
- **Weather pool:** Clear, Ashfall, Breathing Night.
- **Favours:** Bonespire (long sightlines), Thornrunner (tunnel flanks), the Commit pair (open teamfights).

## Map 2 · The Elder Ribs

**Regions:** Bone Flats. **Identity:** siege play around a central fortress
made of the Rememberer's fossil ribcage.

```
                         NORTH BASE
          ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │ ●core  ◇   (H)    ◇    (H)   ◇   ●core   │
          │   ◇    ◇     ◇  ●core  ◇    ◇     ◇      │
 W  ♥     │  ◇   ╔══ ribs ══╗ ◇  ╔══ ribs ══╗   ◇     │     ✶  E
 GRAVE-   │  ◇   ║  THE  ELDER RIBS (H) centre ║  ◇   │  GLASS
 ROOT     │  ◇   ╚══ ribs ══╝ ◇  ╚══ ribs ══╝   ◇     │  FOSSIL
 (grove)  │   ◇    ◇     ◇  ●core  ◇    ◇     ◇      │  FIELD
          │ ●core  ◇   (H)    ◇    (H)   ◇   ●core   │  (crater)
          └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         SOUTH BASE
```

- **The Elder Ribs (centre):** a fossil ribcage the size of a city block. The
  central Hub sits inside it. The gaps between ribs are **six entrances**,
  each narrow enough to defend. Bonevine lets climbers reach the top of the
  ribs, the best vantage point on the map.
- **Bone gathering:** Bone Harvestmen carry bones into nests around the ribs.
  A nest left alone for 5 min becomes an 80-core cache, so both teams have a
  reason to patrol the outside.
- **Glass Fossil Field (east, crater zone):** fossils turned to glass by the
  Murmur. Starshard veins grow inside them.
- **Graveroot (west, Heartwood grove):** where old roots wrap the bones of the
  dead. SAP springs and grasping ground.
- **Memory sites:**
  1. *The Rememberer's Vigil*: inside the ribcage, at the base of the spine.
  2. *The Hushed*: a glass fossil in the crater zone, showing the Murmur's first copies.
  3. *Not the Titan*: a fossil skull that reveals the Ribs weren't the First Titan's.
- **Wildlife:** Marrowhounds (scavenging every fight), Bonewrights and their
  totems, Trundlebacks (climbable high ground), Siege Beetles (lure them into
  enemy Cores), Marrow Mantises around the ribs.
- **Apex:** none native. Patron events wake the **Old Tall** or a **Blighted Wyrm** here.
- **Weather pool:** Clear (salt glare at noon), Shard Storm, Breathing Night.
- **Favours:** Bonespire (rib-top vantage), Titan and Brawler (holding the
  entrances), anyone good at weak points (Trundlebacks, Mantises).

## Map 3 · Breathing Canopy

**Regions:** Rootwilds and Hollow Mire. **Identity:** a tight ambush map
where the forest itself changes the sightlines.

```
                 N  ♥ THE BREATHING VALLEY (grove)
          ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │ ◇ ◇  ◇ ◇   canopy  ◇ ◇ ◇   canopy ◇ ◇ ◇  │
 WEST     │(H) ◇ ●core ◇ ◇  ◇ (H) ◇  ◇ ◇ ●core ◇ (H) │    EAST
 BASE  ═══│ ◇ ◇  ◇ ◇ ◇   CENTRE GLADE ◇ ◇ ◇ ◇  ◇ ◇  │═══ BASE
          │(H) ●core  ◇ ◇   ◇  ◇  ◇ ◇  ◇ ◇ ●core (H) │
          │ ◇ ●core ~~~~ HOLLOW MIRE ~~~~ ●core  ◇   │
          └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                 S  ✶ THE FALLEN SHARD (crater zone, in the mire)
```

- **The breathing mechanic:** every **90 s**, the canopy slowly **rises** for
  30 s (open sightlines, light floods in, Bonespire-friendly) and then
  **falls** (dense shade, short sightlines, ambushes). A visible and audible
  cycle that both teams plan around.
- **Many small nodes** (1–2 cells), so territory changes hands constantly. This is Verdant's best map.
- **The Hollow Mire (south):** deep mud, sound damping and Hush Cicadas. It's
  the stealth route between the two halves.
- **The Fallen Shard (south, crater zone):** a meteor fragment sunk in the mire,
  surrounded by Glasscap fungus and whispering Blight.
- **The Breathing Valley (north, Heartwood grove):** where the Breath's
  rhythm is strongest. Heartbloom grows here.
- **Memory sites:**
  1. *The Breath's Wall*: the first tree of the forest-wall.
  2. *The Quiet Descends*: the edge of the Swallowing Pool in the mire.
- **Wildlife:** Glimmerfoxes, Mossback Grazers, Threadlings, a **Weavemother**
  (webs a contested node), Rootworms, Sapdrinkers, Sapback Aphids (protect or
  destroy), Duskmanes and Strider Cranes in the mire, Hexmaws at its edges.
- **Apex:** the **Eightfold Matron**, in a cave beneath the centre glade; her nest wakes in Phase 3.
- **Weather pool:** Clear, Sap Rain, Rime Fog, Breathing Night.
- **Favours:** Verdant and Hollow (nodes and stealth), Thornrunner (ambush).

## How the three maps differ

| | Ashfall Crossing | The Elder Ribs | Breathing Canopy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fight style | Open teamfights | Siege and defence | Ambush and skirmish |
| Nodes | Large, fewer | Medium, clustered around the Ribs | Small, many |
| Signature mechanic | Grey Migration (6 min) | Bone nests and rib entrances | Canopy breathing (90 s) |
| Native apex | Greyback Colossus | None (patron events only) | Eightfold Matron |
| Lineages favoured | Bonespire, Thornrunner, Commit pair | Bonespire, Titan, Brawler | Verdant, Hollow, Thornrunner |

Every lineage is favoured on at least one map. Verdant and Hollow are
favoured on only one, which is worth checking in playtests.

## Post-launch maps

The three maps named for later in [11](11-atlas.md#proposed-launch-match-maps).
They follow the same rules as the launch maps.

### Map 4 · Fevermouth

**Region:** Cinderveil. **Identity:** fast, dangerous fights around a volcano
that reshapes the map on a timer.

- **The Fevermouth (centre):** a volcano with the central Hub on its lower slope.
- **Eruption cycle:** every **4 minutes** it erupts. Lava runs down **one of
  three channels**; the channel glows and rumbles **20 s** in advance, so both
  teams can see where it's going.
  - Lava burns anyone standing in the channel.
  - Cells the lava crosses become **cooling rock**: neutral and uncapturable
    for 60 s. Each eruption cuts a fresh strip through both teams' territory.
- **Embergrass** fields burn when Titans or Brawlers charge through them.
- **Crater zone (north flank):** *Ashglass Field*, glass made by the meteor's heat.
- **Heartwood grove (south flank):** *Ember Root*, an ancient root that survives the heat.
- **Memory site:** *The Fever*: the planet waking its volcanoes to burn out the infection.
- **Wildlife:** Cinderfangs, Magma Beetles (lava trails), Ashwing Moths.
- **Weather pool:** Clear, Ashfall.
- **Favours:** Brawler and Titan (embergrass charges), Thornrunner (fast rotations between eruptions).

### Map 5 · The Sleeper

**Region:** Rimewastes. **Identity:** slow, tense fights on a frozen lake
above a giant that might be waking.

- **The frozen lake (centre):** the Sleeper is visible deep beneath the ice.
- **Breakable ice:** heavy impacts (Titan slams, Brawler Roll Commit, any
  Stage 3 landing) crack the ice. Cracked ice gives way under the next heavy
  hit. Anyone who falls into the water is **slowed by 40% and takes damage**
  until they climb out. Ice refreezes after 90 s.
- **The Sleeper's pulse:** in Phase 4, the Sleeper's heartbeat sends a
  shockwave every **60 s** that **cracks all the lake ice at once**. The
  final fights happen on a breaking surface.
- **Crater zone (west flank):** *Rimeglass*, frost-Blight creeping over a shard.
- **Heartwood grove (east flank):** *The Warm Spring*, where SAP keeps the ice open.
- **Memory site:** *The Seventh*: the planet growing a seventh answer, then stopping.
- **Wildlife:** Frostfangs, Snowback Grazers, Rime Weavers, Rimeback Stags, Stiltwalkers.
- **Weather pool:** Clear, Rime Fog, Breathing Night.
- **Favours:** Hollow and Verdant (control the dry land and the shore nodes),
  Bonespire (long sightlines across the ice). This helps Verdant and Hollow,
  who were favoured on only one launch map.

### Map 6 · Shard Reef

**Region:** Shattered Coast. **Identity:** a coastal front where the tide
opens and closes parts of the map.

- **The tide:** every **3 minutes** the tide comes in for 60 s. Two low-lying
  nodes on each side flood and become **Inaccessible**. When the tide goes
  out, **tide pools** appear with small core caches.
- **The Shard Reef (centre):** a reef of meteor glass that sings in the wind.
  The central Hub is on it, reached by three causeways that flood at high tide.
- **Stillsong Whales:** a Planet Pulse event unique to this map. Whale song
  stops Blight spreading for 20 s.
- **Crater zone (north flank):** *The Glittering Shallows*, sea water full of Blight.
- **Heartwood grove (south flank):** *Kelp Root*, where the planet's roots reach the sea.
- **Lore note:** the coast *looks* half-blighted (cyan-violet water, shard
  cliffs). In gameplay both teams start equal.
- **Memory sites:** *The Breaker's Stand* (the cracked cliffs) and *The Deep
  Swallows* (the Deepmaw rising to swallow meteor fragments).
- **Wildlife:** Tide Scuttlers, Tidecrawlers, Strider Cranes, Shoal-lights (ambient).
- **Weather pool:** Clear, Shard Storm, Rime Fog.
- **Favours:** Thornrunner and Brawler (causeway fights), Hollow (blinking across flooded ground).

### Map 7 · The Nerve

**Region:** Underroot. Arrives in Season 4. A three-level cave map where
nerve pulses reveal anyone in a corridor every 20 s. Full details in
[30 — Season 4](30-season-4.md#new-map-the-nerve-underroot).
