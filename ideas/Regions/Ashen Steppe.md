# Ashen Steppe

![[region-ashen-steppe.jpg]]

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Landforms | Rolling plains with 3–8 m swells; dry riverbeds 10–30 m wide; the Standing Stones, 12–20 m tall, in a line every 400 m |
| Ground | Ashgrass (knee height, grey-gold), cracked dry earth, ash drifts in hollows |
| Light | Pale and flat. Sky #C9C6BC, ground #8F8A7A. Long shadows at dusk |

## From the atlas

Wind-swept grey grassland under a pale sky. Fine ash drifts constantly. Huge
herds migrate across it.
- **Hazards:** ashfall storms (visibility drops), stampedes.
- **Wildlife:** Ashfangs, Spurlarks, Gravel Skinks, [[Stiltwalker|Stiltwalkers]], [[Ember Moth|Ember Moths]], the Greyback Colossus, the [[Hive Colossus]].
- **Landmark:** **The Grey Migration**, a river of herds that crosses the steppe and can block a battle for a full minute.
- **Map feel:** large nodes, long sightlines, [[Bonespire]] heaven, open teamfights.
