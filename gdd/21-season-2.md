## Finale mode: The Waking

- **Map:** a special version of **The Sleeper**. The Sleeper is breaking free from the ice at the centre of the lake.
- **Objective (same for both sides): Attune.** Six **resonance nodes** ring the
  lake. Holding them fills your side's **Attunement** bar. The Sleeper's
  heartbeat shockwave cracks the ice every 45 s, and when it's fully cracked,
  the lake becomes open water for 15 s, cutting off the centre.
- **Winning a match:** the side with more Attunement when the Sleeper wakes (12 min) wins.
- **Community result:**
  - **The planet attunes first:** the Sleeper wakes **as a Wildborn creature**.
    Stillheart's Season 3 launch trailer and debut Memory show the planet's
    version first; the Blightborn copy appears a week later in the story.
  - **The Murmur attunes first:** the Murmur **touches the Sleeper before it
    fully wakes**. The debut shows the Blightborn copy first, a Hushed Sleeper,
    and the planet's version answers it.
  - **Either way, both sides get Stillheart on the same day** (the mirror rule
    holds). The result changes the story and a debut cosmetic, not gameplay.

## The seventh lineage: Stillheart (draft)

The planet's **first, abandoned answer**: the shape it tried before it split
its power into six. In lore it embodies the planet's **sleep**: slowness,
stillness and patience.

**Role:** tempo controller and support. It slows the fight down for enemies
and buys time for allies.

| Stage | Wildborn look | Blightborn look |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 (L3–9) | A curled, frost-furred creature, slow and soft | The same, with frost replaced by fine glass needles |
| Stage 2 (L10–19) | A long-limbed, ice-antlered creature that moves like a sloth until it suddenly doesn't | Antlers of black glass; its breath leaves glittering frost |
| Stage 3 (L20) | A **walking glacier** with a slow-beating heart visible in its icy chest | A glacier of dark crystal, its heart pulsing cyan-violet |

**Weak point:** the **heart** in its chest, visible at every Stage (easier to
find, so it must be protected).

**Kit (draft):**
- **Passive · Slow Heart:** regenerates faster out of combat. Its sound
  signature is the **quietest** of any large Ascendant.
- **Q · Lull:** a cone that slows enemies by 40% for 2 s. Two Lulls in a row
  make them **Drowse**: asleep for 1 s, broken by any damage.
- **E · Hibernate:** put **yourself or an ally** into ice stasis for 2 s.
  Invulnerable, but unable to act. A carrying ally keeps all their cores. The
  perfect save for a player who's carrying a lot on the way home.
- **R · Long Night:** an area where **enemy cooldowns tick 30% slower** for 5 s.
- **Stage 3 Ultimate · The Deep Sleep:** a huge area where **all node capture
  progress is frozen** for 6 s. Nothing can be captured or lost inside it.

**Stats (L20 proposal):** Health 3,600 · Power 150 · Speed 90 · Control 120.

### Pairing: universal partner (decided)

**Decided (2026-10-02): Stillheart is a permanent universal partner.** It
never gets a Synergy ability of its own. Instead it strengthens whoever it
fights beside, which fits its lore as the answer from *before* the planet
split its power into six.

**Lullaby Resonance (base):** an ally within 15 m has their Synergy cooldowns
reduced by **10%**.

**Attunement (proposal, to give the pick a strong identity):** once per life,
Stillheart can **attune** to one ally (2 s channel). Until either dies, it
gets a small bonus tuned to that ally's lineage:

| Attuned to | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Titan | Lull also roots slammed targets for 0.5 s |
| Brawler | Hibernate on the Brawler ends with a free Roll Commit |
| Verdant | Long Night also slows enemy capture speed by 30% |
| Hollow | Drowse lasts 0.5 s longer inside a Void Rift |
| Thornrunner | Marked targets are also Lulled |
| Bonespire | Drowsing targets take 2× weak-point damage from the Bonespire's next lance |

This keeps Stillheart flexible in the draft while giving each partnership its own flavour.

## How Season 2 feels

Season 1 showed players that the world has a story. Season 2 makes the story
**personal**: something is waking that will become a creature they can play.

It's the coldest season. Frost forms once per match on every map, then a
little more each week. On The Sleeper, a giant shape lies under the ice of
the lake, and its heartbeat is a sound players learn to listen for: faint
in week 1, unmistakable by week 7. When the crack appears across the lake,
players watch it grow from match to match, and the Blighted creatures that
gather at its edge, perfectly still and listening, are one of the most
unsettling images the game has shown.

The mood is **anticipation**, and a little fear. The planet is waking
something it once chose to put to sleep. Why?

## A moment on The Sleeper

Phase 4. A Wildborn Titan steps onto the lake to reach the central nodes.
Under its feet, deep down, the Sleeper's heart beats once. Sixty seconds
since the last pulse. The Titan's player knows what comes next. The shockwave
rolls out from the centre of the lake and the ice cracks everywhere at once.
The Titan plunges through into freezing water, slowed and hurting. On the
shore, a Blightborn Bonespire, who stayed on solid ground and counted the
seconds, lances its glowing back crystal from fifty metres away.

## The Waking, walked through

**0:00–3:00.** Both broods bud on opposite shores. The lake is frozen solid.
Six resonance nodes ring it. Teams split to grab the nearest ones.

**3:00–6:00.** The Sleeper's heartbeat begins its 45-second cycle. Every pulse
cracks the ice further. Teams learn to cross the lake only between pulses.

**6:00–9:00.** The ice is fully cracked. Every pulse now turns the lake to open
water for 15 seconds, cutting the map in half. Holding nodes on both halves
becomes the challenge.

**9:00–12:00.** The Sleeper's shape rises closer to the surface. Its eye opens
beneath the ice, glowing. The Attunement bars race.

**12:00.** The Sleeper wakes. The team with more Attunement sees it first:
frost-furred and amber-eyed, or glazed in glass and violet-eyed.

## The Rime Turn in this season

- Frost and cold light on every map; Rimewastes variants everywhere.
- The Murmur's lore advantage in Rime makes the race to the Sleeper feel urgent.
- The Sleeper's heartbeat is audible not only on its own map, but faintly under every frozen surface in the game.

## Writing guidance

1. **Keep Stillheart's purpose hidden** this season. Players should wonder
   why the planet feared it; Season 6 answers ([49](49-season-6.md#the-reveal-why-stillheart-sleeps)).
2. **Make the Sleeper feel vast and patient,** not monstrous.
3. **Let the Murmur's interest be curious, not hungry:** it wants to learn the Sleeper's shape.
4. **The waking should feel like birth,** not like a monster breaking free.

## Fevermouth's arrival mid-season

In week 5, the volcano map arrives, and with it a contrast: Season 2 is a cold
season, but the Fevermouth burns. The Memory unlocked that week (the planet
trying a seventh shape, then letting it sleep) plays out against both
settings in players' minds: ice where the planet hid its answer, fire where
it fights. The map's eruption cycle teaches timing, the same skill the
Sleeper's pulses teach on the lake.

## Stillheart's first appearance

Stillheart isn't playable until Season 3, but players see it in Season 2:

- **Week 1:** only a shape under the ice.
- **Week 5:** its silhouette in the Memory *Why It Slept*.
- **Week 10:** the finale's waking: the first full view of the creature, as Wildborn or Blightborn first.
- **Between seasons:** the Den shows a frost-covered shape curled near the
  crack in the roof, a teaser that disappears when Season 3 begins and Stillheart arrives.

## What Season 2 sets up

- **Stillheart** (Season 3): the seventh lineage and universal partner.
- **The planet's secret** (Season 6): why it feared what it made.
- **The Murmur's curiosity:** its race to copy the Sleeper shows it wants to
  learn every shape, which leads to its first creation in Season 4.

## Community result, presented

At the end of the finale week:

1. The War Map frosts over completely for a moment.
2. A short cinematic: the lake's ice shatters, and the Sleeper rises.
3. Depending on the result, it rises frost-furred and amber-eyed (the planet's
   version first) or glazed and violet-eyed (the Murmur's copy first).
4. The other version appears behind it, a moment later.
5. The Waking Memory unlocks for everyone.

## A note on Stillheart's balance at launch

Stillheart arrives as a new lineage with no pair, and new lineages are often
too strong or too weak at first. For its first season, its numbers should be
tuned slightly conservative, with a bonus Mastery track to encourage players
to try it. Its Attunement bonuses should be watched especially closely.

## Closing note

Season 2 is the season where the world gives players something new to *be*.
Every system in it (the cold, the pulses, the race, the waking) should build
toward the moment Stillheart opens its eyes.
